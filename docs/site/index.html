Ivy Vaults

Deposit assets to back one option, earn its premium, and claim what remains when the option ends.

An Ivy vault is a smart contract for a single option trade. The depositors’ final claim depends on the option’s terms and whether the buyer exercises. Selling another option requires a new vault.

The people around a vault

OWNER

Sets the terms

The owner creates the vault, chooses its tokens and sets the trading terms. The owner funds it alone or allows other depositors.

For a call, the owner can allow bids in several currencies. For a put, bids must use the deposited token as their strike currency. The owner makes these token choices when creating the vault.

LIQUIDITY PROVIDER

Supplies collateral

The LP deposits collateral and receives shares.

Whoever holds shares when the option activates can claim their premium immediately, after the platform fee. They do not have to wait for exercise or expiry.

After settlement, the current shareholder burns shares to claim their portion of the remaining assets.

BUYER

Signs the bid

The buyer signs a bid within the owner's allowed terms and must hold MARKET_MAKER_ROLE at activation.

BID MASTER

Selects & submits

The bid master runs the auction off-chain, selects a buyer's signed bid and submits it to the Hub for activation.

CUSTODY

The individual vault

Holds the tokens and reserves. Token approvals target this address.

Executor and recipient

The buyer can submit transactions and receive the assets in the same wallet. Separate addresses are optional.

Executor
Delegate exercise and payout claims to an operator or bot. For physical exercise, that caller must supply and approve the payment tokens.
Recipient
Send the assets directly to a treasury, multisig or integration contract. The buyer can update this address if their receiving wallet changes.
Future layers
A swap adapter could receive the vault's output and route converted tokens to the buyer, for example through a future NEAR integration. The adapter would provide the swap and routing logic; setting a recipient alone does not perform a swap.

Only the buyer can update the executor and recipient. An executor cannot change where assets are sent.

PHASE 1
Auction
  • LPsThe committed collateral stays fixed during the auction, so LPs cannot deposit or withdraw and the owner cannot change the terms. If the admin enables transfers, shares remain transferable even though collateral cannot leave through a withdrawal.
  • Bid masterThe bid master runs the auction off-chain and submits the chosen buyer's signed bid for activation. The contracts check that bid against the vault's terms before accepting it.
Exit activate(vaultId, bid, signature). Cancel back to Open: bid master any time, owner after its timeout recorded when the vault was created, which is 3 days by default, or immediately when admission is paused or expiry has passed.
PHASE 2
Live
  • Market makerexercise(vaultId, amount) inside its window, for part or all of the remaining notional, subject to its fixed partial-exercise policy, exercise style and settlement type.
  • LPsPremium claims are available immediately. Shares can be transferred when enabled, and holders can consent to an early exit by agreement.
Exit expire(vaultId) by anyone at the expiration time; also finalized automatically on full exercise, or through a buyer-signed unanimous unwind.
PHASE 3
Settled
  • Shareholdersclaim(vaultId, shares) burns shares for a pro-rata slice of unreserved collateral and proceeds. Premium and buyer reserves are excluded.
  • Market makerclaimPayout(vaultId) collects a reserved buyer payout for the configured recipient: a separately funded unwind refund, or an owed cash-expiry payout for a cash-settled vault. Physical exercise pays the recipient in the exercise transaction.
The vault is finished. Claims remain available, but the vault cannot sell another option. Continuing the strategy requires creating and funding a new vault.
Admission pause. The guardian is authorized to pause admission globally or per vault. Pauses block new creation where applicable, deposits, auction opening and activation. Withdrawals, transfers, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwinds remain available. Deadlines never move.

Cancelling an auction returns the vault to Open and clears any scheduled start time, so the owner must schedule again or open manually. Every other transition is one-way.

Snapshot
Exercised notional, share supply, refund, nonce and a future deadline. Execution must still find the vault Live.
Consent changes
Holders may revoke. A nonzero transfer to another holder invalidates both sender and recipient approvals. They approve again at their new balances.
New agreement
A replacement proposal or further exercise requires fresh agreement. After expiry or replacement, propose again while Live with a new future deadline; the contract assigns the next nonce, and old signatures and approvals cannot execute it.
Funding
Each current LP approves the vault clone and funds a proportional contribution in the premium token, rounded up to a raw unit. Zero refund needs the same consent but no refund funding.
Separate claims
Buyer or executor calls claimPayout for the configured recipient. Shareholders claim the residual pool and use claimPremium separately, even after burning every share.
Preserved
Earned premium and completed exercises remain intact. A proposal suspends neither exercise nor expiration.

Unwind procedure and commands ↗

Premium on cancellation and pause

Auction cancelled before activation

No premium or fee collected; nothing to refund. The vault returns to Open for withdrawals.

Unwind consent revoked, proposal expired or replaced

Earned premium and fees stay unchanged. The option continues, and funders can recover their deposits.

Unwind executed

LPs retain earned premium; the treasury retains fees. Current LPs separately fund their proportional negotiated premium-token refund. The buyer or executor claims it for the recipient.

Admission paused

Earned premium and claims stay unchanged. Exercise, expiration and agreed unwind remain available; deadlines do not move.

Normal exercise or expiration

Earned premium and fees remain claimable. Settlement payments are separate obligations.

Refund funding has its own recovery path

  1. 01

    Before execution

    A funder can recover the deposit. Withdrawing also revokes current consent.

  2. 02

    After execution

    Only excess contributions and allocated ceiling surplus can be recovered.

  3. 03

    Final recovery

    The last surplus participant receives rounding dust. The buyer receives exactly the agreed refund.

Original funder
Recovery stays attached to the funder after share transfers or burns. Claimed premium never excuses a current LP’s refund contribution.
Admission pause
The implemented pause stops admission, not existing obligations. A full execution freeze would need a separate specification covering permissions, actions, deadlines, resumption and payments.

Optional cash settlement

Cash payouts use the vault’s collateral token and the Hub’s stored settlement prices. The feature is implemented but disabled by default; the rules below apply after the administrator enables it. Physical delivery needs no settlement publisher.

Two independent switches

Physical launch · cash admissions disabled · no publisher
  1. 01

    Arrange publication

    Agree methodology, evidence and operator. Choose an EOA, multisig or authenticated helper.

  2. 02

    Grant the role

    Admin grants SETTLEMENT_PRICE_PUBLISHER_ROLE on the Hub.

  3. 03

    Enable admissions

    Admin calls setCashSettlementEnabled(true). Cash/Either vaults require positive maxSettlementPriceAge.

Publication route
EOAs submit directly; helpers use the same APIs. No settlement feed is required, and the Hub does not read prices back from a helper.
Independence
Granting or revoking a role never toggles the feature. The on-chain flag setter does not check publisher coverage. The operator command preflights a nominated publisher; the admin remains responsible for publication.
When disabled
New Cash/Either vaults and cash bids, including pending auctions, are blocked. Physical bids may still activate, including in existing Either vaults.
Existing obligations
Physical vaults keep their delivery method. Existing cash positions retain publication, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwind; disabling removes none of their payment obligations.

Cash enablement procedure ↗

A hard boundary at expiry

BEFORE EXPIRY

Latest exercise observation

publishExercisePrice(vaultId, price, observedAt, validUntil)
  • American cash exercise only.
  • Positive price; validity deadline not yet passed. The vault's maximum age is checked when the buyer exercises, not at publication.
  • Each new observation timestamp must be strictly later.
t = expiry↓
AT OR AFTER EXPIRY

Write-once final price

publishExpiry(vaultId, price, validUntil)
  • Publish at or after fixed expiry.
  • Even a fresh exercise observation is no longer enough.
  • Once finalized, usable indefinitely.
One vault per report
Every cash vault needs independent transactions keyed by vaultId, even with matching pairs and expiries. The Hub derives underlying, selected quote and fixed expiry from the Live position.
Publisher scope
Any role holder may publish for any eligible cash vault. There is no per-vault assignment or built-in quorum.
Units & authority
Quote-token units per whole underlying. The indicative priceFeed only constrains activation. No indicative-price or other-vault fallback can authorize cash payment.
Methodology
Operators calculate the proposed 30-minute average ending at exact expiry off-chain. Production sources, sampling and calculations need approval before enablement. The Hub verifies authority and time bindings, not market-data accuracy.
Irreversible
Final expiry prices cannot be corrected or deleted. Revoking a publisher erases neither its reports nor completed payments. Verify the calculation and vault ID before submission.

Cash exercise, expiration and claims

EXERCISE

Vault → recipient

Buyer or executor calls exercise(vaultId, amount). Intrinsic value is paid immediately.

Zero payout → revert

Partial policy applies. Full exercise finalizes the vault.

EXPIRATION

Vault → buyer reserve → recipient

At or after expiry, anyone calls expire(vaultId); buyer or executor later uses claimPayout(vaultId).

Zero payout → may finalize

Remaining notional is finalized. Unreserved assets go to shareholder claims; premium stays separate.

Cash expiration needs an on-chain transaction and that vault’s final expiry price. There is no physical-delivery grace window; remaining buyer value is reserved instead of lapsing.
