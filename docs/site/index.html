Single-use · Covered calls & cash-secured puts · Physical or cash

A vault sells exactly one option.

LPs deposit collateral into a new vault. An off-chain auction picks a market maker. The bid master activates the winning bid on-chain, and the premium moves into the vault in that same transaction. Activation holders can claim premium immediately. The buyer or its executor may exercise within the agreed window; after finalization, current shareholders claim the unreserved pool. A vault is never reused.

01
Collateral and shares

What goes in, and what you hold.

collateral == underlying

Covered call

  • Deposit the underlying, for example WETH.
  • Bids quote strike in a listed quote token and premium in that pair’s fixed premium token.
  • Notional equals the collateral. 10 WETH covers 10 calls.
collateral != underlying

Cash-secured put

  • Deposit the quote token, for example USDC. The underlying is fixed at creation.
  • One pair only.
  • Notional is collateral × 10^decimals / strike. 30,000 USDC at a 3,000 strike covers 10 puts.
Shares
One share is one smallest unit of collateral, minted on the amount actually received. ERC-1155 on IvyShares, token id = vault id, transferable in every phase. Current holders claim the remaining pool after settlement; activation premium stays with its original holder.
Kind
Read from the two addresses at creation. Same address means covered call, different means cash-secured put. Nobody declares it.
Approvals
Approve the vault address, never the hub. The hub tells the vault to pull and holds no tokens. deposit(amount) on the vault works too.
Premium
Pulled at activation and immediately claimable through claimPremium(vaultId). Each activation holder keeps its entitlement after transfers or burns. Floor-rounding dust stays reserved permanently.

Lifecycle

Four phases, one direction.

PHASE 0
Open

  • OwnerMay only tighten terms, schedule the auction, transfer ownership.
Exit openAuction by the owner, or by anyone once auctionStartsAt has passed. Needs at least minCollateral and a future exact expiry.
PHASE 1
Auction
  • LPsFunds frozen. No deposits, withdrawals or term changes.
  • Bid masterRuns the auction off-chain and submits the winner.
Exit activate(vaultId, bid, signature). Cancel back to Open: bid master any time, owner after its snapshotted timeout (3 days by default), or immediately when admission is paused or expiry has passed.
PHASE 2
Live
  • Market makerexercise(vaultId, amount) inside its window, any amount up to what remains, as often as it likes.
  • LPsClaim activation premium now. Shares still transfer; current holders may vote on an unwind.
Exit settle(vaultId) by anyone at the settlement time, automatically on full exercise, or through a buyer-signed unanimous unwind.
PHASE 3
Settled
  • Share holdersclaim(vaultId, shares) burns shares for a pro-rata slice of unreserved collateral and proceeds. Premium and buyer reserves are excluded.
  • Market makerclaimPayout(vaultId) collects a reserved cash payout or unwind refund for the configured recipient.
Terminal. Create a new vault to roll.
Admission pause. Global or per-vault pauses block new creation where applicable, deposits, auction opening and activation. Withdrawals, transfers, exercise, reports, settlement, claims and agreed unwinds remain available. Deadlines never move.

05
Market makers

Bidding and exercise.

An auction ID, exact collateral amount, accepted pair hash, expiry and execution settings are signed into every bid. Reopening an auction requires a new bid. A Live vault can end early through an agreement signed by the buyer and approved by all current shares. The executing sponsor funds the agreed refund in the premium token; completed exercises and earned premium are preserved.

  1. Sign the bidEIP-712, domain IvyVaultsHub / 2 / chain id / deployed hub. Needs MARKET_MAKER_ROLE. Nonces are free-form: sign many, the winner is consumed, the rest lapse at validUntil or via cancelBid(nonce).
  2. Approve the vaultFor the total premium, floor(premium × notional / underlyingUnit). For physical exercise the executing buyer or executor approves and supplies the consideration.
  3. ActivationThe bid master submits your bid unchanged. The vault pulls the premium in the same transaction.
  4. ExerciseInside your window (Fig. 04), any amount up to what remains. Cash payouts come out of collateral. Only the buyer may change its executor or recipient; exercise assets go only to that recipient.
struct Bid {
  uint256 vaultId;
  address marketMaker;
  address quoteToken;
  uint256 strike;       // quote per 1 underlying
  uint256 premium;      // premiumToken per 1 underlying
  uint8   style;        // 0 European, 1 American
  uint8   settlement;   // 0 Physical, 1 Cash
  uint64  expiry;       // unix seconds
  uint64  validUntil;   // signature deadline
  uint256 nonce;
  uint256 auctionId;
  uint256 collateralAmount;
  bytes32 pairHash;
  address executor;
  address recipient;
}

For raw token amounts, underlyingUnit = 10 ** underlyingDecimals. Strike and premium are raw payment-token units per whole underlying.

Expiry reports are final. Cash settlement uses Ivy’s signed 30-minute average ending at the exact expiry. The feed verifies the signature, not the averaging calculation. Reports are write-once and support multisig contract signers. If Ivy cannot publish, cash vaults remain locked until a valid report arrives or the buyer and all current shareholders consent to an unwind. There is no stale-price or zero-payout fallback.
06
Developers

Six contracts and two fixed libraries.

Immutable hubIvyVaultsHub

Creates vaults, verifies bids and runs the phase machine. Linked libraries implement term rules and settlement logic. Every token-moving call is nonReentrant.

EIP-1167 cloneIvyVault

One per vault. Holds tokens and token-keyed reserves. Ordinary hub transfers spend only unreserved assets; premium payments also obey an independent fixed budget.

ERC-1155IvyShares

LP shares. Only the hub may mint or burn. Pre-update notifications checkpoint activation balances and invalidate unwind votes before balances or receiver callbacks change.

Immutable signerIvyPriceFeed

Separate signed fresh spot and finalized expiry lookups. Prices use quote decimals per whole underlying. Historical prices remain valid for delayed settlement.

Activation incomeIvyPremiums

Lazy checkpoints preserve premium ownership. Payments can only start through the guarded hub claim; the vault enforces the premium budget independently.

Shareholder consentIvyUnwind

One active agreement per vault. Transfers invalidate affected votes, replacement supersedes approvals, and normal exercise or settlement remains available.

Linked rules libraryIvyVaultRules

Creation validation and term tightening. Creation checks admission. Tightening checks the owner and Open phase.

Linked settlement libraryIvyOptionSettlement

Exercise, cash settlement and residual claims execute against hub storage, under its reentrancy guard. Library addresses are fixed in hub bytecode.

Calls by phaseRoles · DEFAULT_ADMIN · GUARDIAN · BID_MASTER · MARKET_MAKER

Two libraries deploy first, followed by the vault implementation, hub, shares, premium module, unwind module and signed feed. Addresses are predicted from a fixed nonce sequence; deployment and recovery verify constructor bindings and every embedded library link. There are no upgrade, library or module replacement setters. Admins retain role, metadata and future-default controls; guardians can pause admission.
