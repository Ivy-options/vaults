Single-use · Covered calls & cash-secured puts · Physical or cash

A vault sells exactly one option.

LPs deposit collateral into a new vault. An off-chain auction picks a market maker. The bid master activates the winning bid on-chain, and the premium moves into the vault in that same transaction. At expiry the market maker exercises or does not. Share holders then claim their share of whatever the vault holds. A vault is never reused.

01
Collateral and shares

What goes in, and what you hold.

collateral == underlying

Covered call

Deposit the underlying, for example WETH. Bids quote strike and premium in one of the vault's quote tokens (USDC, DAI). Notional equals the collateral, so 10 WETH covers 10 calls.

The hub reads the kind from these two addresses at creation. Nobody declares it.

collateral != underlying

Cash-secured put

Deposit the quote token, for example USDC. The underlying is fixed at creation and the vault has one pair. Notional depends on the winning strike: collateral × 10^decimals / strike. 30,000 USDC at a 3,000 strike covers 10 puts.

Strike is always quote units per one whole underlying, e.g. 3000e6 USDC per WETH.

Shares. One share is one smallest unit of collateral. The hub mints shares for the amount the vault actually received, so fee-on-transfer tokens are safe. Shares are ERC-1155 tokens on IvyShares and the token id is the vault id. They transfer freely in every phase, and whoever holds them at settlement claims. Premium is part of the pot, so there is no separate premium claim.

Approvals. Approve the vault address, never the hub. The hub tells the vault to pull and never holds tokens itself. You can also call deposit(amount) on the vault directly. It notifies the hub.

Lifecycle

Four phases, one direction.

Fig. 02 · Who may act in each phase
PHASE 0
Open
Anyone
Deposit and withdraw. Non-owners only if publicDeposits is on.
Owner
May tighten terms, schedule the auction, transfer ownership. Opens the auction with openAuction. Anyone may open it once auctionStartsAt has passed. Needs collateral of at least minCollateral.
PHASE 1
Auction
Funds
Frozen. Deposits, withdrawals and tightening revert.
Bid master
Activates the off-chain winner with activate(vaultId, bid, signature). May cancel back to Open at any time. The owner may cancel after auctionTimeout, 3 days by default.
PHASE 2
Live
Market maker
Calls exercise(vaultId, amount) inside its window, as often as it likes, up to the remaining notional. The premium is already in the vault.
Anyone
Calls settle(vaultId) once the settlement time arrives. A full exercise settles on its own.
PHASE 3
Settled
Share holders
claim(vaultId, shares) burns shares and pays shares / totalShares of every token in the vault.
Market maker
Collects a cash payout with claimPayout. The vault is finished. Create a new one to roll.
Cancelling an auction returns the vault to Open and clears its schedule. Every other transition is one-way.
03
Outcomes

What the pot contains at settlement.

Symbols: D call collateral, C put collateral, K strike, N = C / K put notional, S spot at settlement, f exercised fraction. P is the total premium, premium × notional. The market maker pays it at activation, so it is always in the pot.

OutcomeCovered call (deposit D underlying)Cash-secured put (deposit C quote)
Not exercisedD underlying + PC quote + P
Physical, fraction f(1−f)·D underlying + f·D·K quote + Pf·N underlying + C − f·N·K quote + P
Cash, unexercised rD − r·(S−K)/S underlying + P (payout 0 if S ≤ K)C − r·(K−S) quote + P (payout 0 if S ≥ K)

Rounding favours LPs. The market maker pays ceil and receives floor, and remainders stay in the vault for the pro-rata split. Cash payouts come out of the collateral, so the market maker delivers nothing at a cash exercise.

05
Market makers

Bidding and exercise.

The bid

A bid is EIP-712 typed data. The domain is IvyVaultsHub, version 1, the chain id and the hub proxy address. The signer must hold MARKET_MAKER_ROLE. Nonces are free-form, so you can sign several competing bids. Activation consumes the winner. The rest lapse at validUntil, or you burn them with cancelBid(nonce). The bid master submits your bid unchanged.

At activation the vault pulls the total premium, premium × notional / 10^decimals, from you. Approve the vault for it beforehand. For physical settlement, also approve what you will pay at exercise.

struct Bid {
  uint256 vaultId;
  address marketMaker;
  address quoteToken;
  uint256 strike;       // quote per 1 underlying
  uint256 premium;      // premiumToken per 1 underlying
  uint8   style;        // 0 European, 1 American
  uint8   settlement;   // 0 Physical, 1 Cash
  uint64  expiry;       // unix seconds
  uint64  validUntil;   // signature deadline
  uint256 nonce;
}

Dead-feed rule. Cash settle reverts with StalePrice while the feed is stale or unavailable. After expiry + settlementGracePeriod (7 days) the hub accepts a stale price, and a feed that reverts settles with zero payout. An oracle can never lock LP funds.

06
Developers

Four contracts, three roles.

UUPS proxyIvyVaultsHub

Creates vaults, holds every rule, verifies bids, runs the phase machine. Every token-moving call is nonReentrant.

EIP-1167 cloneIvyVault

One per vault. Holds tokens and nothing else. Anyone may deposit. Only the hub may pull or push.

ERC-1155IvyShares

LP shares. Only the hub may mint or burn. Lives outside the hub to stay under the 24,576-byte limit.

InterfaceIIvyPriceFeed

spot(underlying, quote) returns (price, updatedAt), price in quote decimals per one whole underlying. You implement the feed.
