    Ivy Vaults

    Before the vault

    Deposit assets to back one option, earn its premium, and claim what remains when the option ends. An Ivy vault is a smart contract for a single option trade. The buyer pays an upfront premium for the right to trade an agreed asset at an agreed price, the strike. Using that right is exercising. Depositors provide the backing, so what they receive depends on whether the buyer exercises. Selling another option requires a new vault.

    For example, an option on WETH might have a strike of 3,000 USDC per WETH. WETH is the underlying token; USDC is the quote token. A call gives the buyer the right to buy WETH at the strike; a put gives the right to sell it.

    With physical settlement, exercising exchanges those tokens. With cash settlement, the buyer receives the calculated payoff from the assets deposited in the vault instead.

    Who is around a vault

    LPs supply the seller's collateral and receive shares. A market maker buys the option. The owner manages the vault, the bid master selects the offer, and the protocol administrator is a separate role.

    Sets the terms

    The owner creates the vault and sets its tokens and trading terms. The owner may also be an LP and can fund the vault alone, accept deposits from other LPs, or both.

    Supplies collateral

    The LP deposits collateral, the asset set aside to meet the option's payment obligations, and receives shares in one vault. Shares determine its part of the premium and remaining assets.

    Buys and manages the option

    The market maker is the option buyer and manages the position through settlement. It proposes the premium and terms in a signed bid within the owner's limits, pays the premium at activation, and holds the right to exercise.

    Selects and submits

    The bid master runs the auction off-chain, selects a buyer's signed bid and submits it to the Hub for activation.

    Runs the protocol settings

    The protocol administrator manages global settings and permissions. That is separate from owning a vault.

    How instructions reach a vault

    Owner and bid master instructions go to the shared Hub, which validates them and calls the individual vault. A market maker's bid reaches the Hub through the bid master. Pick a character to see the route.

    Who calls what

      How custody works

      The Hub validates instructions, collects the premium and moves the vault to Live. The individual vault holds the tokens and reserves.

      One Hub, separate vaults

      One shared Hub coordinates calls to separate vaults. Each vault has its own ID and its own contract address, readable from the creation receipt or the Hub getters.

      The Hub holds nothing

      No token custody. Even the premium the Hub collects is held in the vault, as a reserve.

      Approvals target the vault

      Token approvals name the individual vault's address, never the Hub.

      pull and push

      The Hub calls the vault's pull(...) so the vault receives tokens, and the vault's push(...) to send them.

      Two deposit routes

      Deposit calls can use the Hub or vault.deposit(amount) directly. Both follow the same admission rules.

      Who may act for the buyer

      The buyer can use one wallet for the whole trade: it submits transactions and receives the assets. To split those jobs, the buyer can choose an executor, a recipient, or both.

      Executor and recipient

      An executor submits exercise and payout-claim transactions for the buyer. A recipient is the address that receives the buyer's assets.

      Executor

      An operator or bot. For physical exercise it must supply and approve the tokens that pay the vault.

      Recipient

      For example, an operator submits while the vault sends assets straight to the buyer's treasury or multisig.

      Only the buyer changes them

      The buyer uses set-execution; an executor cannot change where assets are sent. A zero executor address revokes delegation.

      A future swap adapter

      A recipient adapter could swap and route tokens, for example through NEAR. This is not implemented; setting a recipient alone does not swap.

      What a pause means

      The guardian, an address with permission to pause new activity, can stop new commitments across the protocol or for one vault. This is called an admission pause.

      Pause new commitments

      A pause blocks new commitments but lets existing positions run under their normal rules, on their normal deadlines.

      What it blocks

      New creation where applicable, deposits, auction opening and activation.

      What it never stops

      Withdrawals, transfers, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwinds that meet their normal rules.

      Deadlines do not move

      A pause does not extend any deadline.

      How to pause

      pause with vaultId: "0" for global admission, or a specific ID for one vault. A paused vault must be unpaused before another deposit or auction.

      Open

      No option has been sold yet. This is the stage for funding the vault and preparing the terms offered to buyers.

      What the vault holds

      I · Backing
      Deposited collateral: the vault's current balance of the chosen collateral token.
      II · Premium and fees
      Empty: no buyer payment has been collected.

      Owner creates the vault

      Before LPs deposit, the owner creates the vault and defines which trades it may accept. Its expiry is an exact timestamp fixed at creation and never moves.

      Set the terms

      The owner chooses the tokens, expiry and exercise rules. A call vault can require a minimum strike and a minimum premium; a buyer's offer must meet both. While Open, some limits can become stricter; LP protections cannot be relaxed, and immutable fields cannot change.

      Three token roles

      Underlying, quote and premium. Collateral is whichever of the first two LPs deposit: 10 WETH for a covered call, 30,000 USDC for a cash-secured put at a 3,000 strike.

      Pairs

      A call may list several quote/premium pairs; a put accepts exactly one pair whose quote token is the collateral. The premium token may be the underlying, the quote, or a third token.

      Physical or cash

      Physical at launch. Cash or Either require the optional cash feature enabled at creation; physical-only terms can never be loosened to cash.

      Every term, one by one

      The full list of terms, what activation checks for each, and what the owner may still change, is on the Vault terms shelf.

      LPs deposit collateral

      While Open, LPs add collateral to the vault and receive shares, or return shares to take collateral back.

      Add funds

      The LP deposits the collateral token. Deposits must not be paused, and by default only the owner may deposit.

      Who may deposit

      Others can deposit only if the vault was created with publicDeposits enabled. That choice is immutable.

      Shares

      Shares determine an LP's part of the premium and of whatever remains at the end.

      Approve, then deposit

      Each depositor runs approve-token, then deposit with the same raw amount. Approvals target the vault, never the Hub.

      Take funds back

      An LP can withdraw some or all of its shares. The contract burns them, because they no longer represent a stake in the vault. This route is available only while Open.

      Owner may tighten the terms

      While Open, the owner can keep or strengthen LP protections. For example, it can raise the minimum premium a buyer must offer, but cannot lower it after LPs have committed their funds.

      Tighten terms

      For example: raise a call's strike limit or lower a put's, disable a pair, or lower the permitted in-the-money distance. None of these protections can be moved the other way.

      Hand over ownership

      Management of the vault can be transferred to another address. There is no dedicated CLI command; use the ABI.

      Owner opens or schedules the auction

      Opening the auction moves the vault from Open to Auction. The owner decides when: now, or from a scheduled start time.

      Open or schedule

      Open manually with openAuction, or set auctionStartsAt. Zero means only the owner may open.

      Conditions

      Admissions unpaused, a nonzero collateral balance at or above minCollateral, and expiry still in the future.

      Anyone may open on schedule

      Once a nonzero auctionStartsAt is reached, anyone can submit the opening transaction. A schedule does not submit it automatically.

      Auction

      The auction is open, and the trade terms are locked. The owner cannot change them while buyers are bidding.

      What the vault holds

      I · Backing
      Committed collateral: the collateral balance committed to this auction.
      II · Premium and fees
      Empty: no buyer payment has been collected.

      Auction opens and terms lock

      Opening the auction locks the trade terms while buyers bid. The auction ID and total shares are read after opening, because every bid commits to both.

      Collateral stays put

      The committed collateral stays fixed, so LPs cannot deposit or withdraw. If the admin enables transfers, shares remain transferable.

      Market makers sign bids off-chain

      The auction happens off-chain. A buyer signs an offer specifying the price, premium and option rules for this auction, and the bid master compares the offers.

      Sign a bid

      The buyer signs its offer as one EIP-712 message. The fields let the Hub check that the submitted bid is exactly the offer the buyer authorized.

      The auction and buyer
      • vaultId uint256: the vault being bid on.
      • auctionId uint256: this auction round.
      • collateralAmount uint256: the committed collateral amount.
      • marketMaker address: the option buyer.
      Tokens and price
      • quoteToken address: the token used to quote the strike.
      • pairHash bytes32: a hash binding the bid to the exact accepted token-pair settings.
      • strike uint256: raw quote-token units per whole underlying.
      • premium uint256: raw premium-token units per whole underlying.
      Timing and option rules
      • style uint8: 0 European, exercise starts at expiry; 1 American, it may start before.
      • settlement uint8: 0 physical at launch; 1 cash, when enabled.
      • expiry uint64: the fixed expiry, a Unix timestamp.
      • validUntil uint64: the latest time this bid may activate, separate from expiry.
      Use and execution
      • nonce uint256: chosen by the buyer. Once used at activation or cancelled, no bid with that nonce can activate.
      • executor address: may exercise or claim a buyer payout for the buyer.
      • recipient address: receives the exercise assets or reserved payout.
      Approve the vault for the premium

      The buyer lets the individual vault collect the full premium: floor(premium × notional / underlyingUnit). For a put, notional is floor(collateral × underlyingUnit / strike).

      underlyingUnit = 10 ** underlyingDecimals, the raw units in one whole underlying token. Approval only grants permission; activation collects.

      Needs the market maker role

      The buyer needs MARKET_MAKER_ROLE for activation. Signing alone does not open a position.

      Cancel an unused bid

      cancelBid(nonce) stops an unused bid with that nonce, not the auction or a sold option. A reopened auction gets a new ID, so the buyer signs again.

      Bid master activates the winning bid

      The bid master selects one signed bid and submits it. The vault checks it and collects the premium in the same transaction.

      Submit for activation

      The bid master calls activate(vaultId, bid, signature). The offer must fit the vault's terms, and the buyer must have enough funds and token allowance to pay the premium.

      If the checks pass

      Signature, terms and funding all pass: the premium is collected and the option becomes Live.

      If any check fails

      The whole transaction is rejected. The vault stays in Auction, and that attempt collects no premium.

      Optional market price check

      The owner can limit how far a strike may differ in the buyer's favor from the spot price in priceFeed, by maxInTheMoneyBps.

      Callsstrike ≥ spot·(10000−bps)/10000Putsstrike ≤ spot·(10000+bps)/10000

      Both this and strikeLimit must pass. A report older than maxPriceAge seconds rejects activation.

      Simulate, then submit

      inspect-bid takes the real signature and simulates activation as the bid master: the role, the signature, the policies, the oracle band and whether the premium is funded. Then submit activate with the same signed bid.

      If cancelled, back to Open

      Cancelling an auction is the only way to return to an earlier phase. The vault goes back to Open, with its collateral still inside.

      Cancel at any time

      The bid master can cancel the auction at any time, with no waiting period.

      Cancel after the timeout

      The owner can cancel too, with cancel-auction, but normally has to wait until the auction has been open for a set time.

      How long the owner waits

      Three days by default: an auctionTimeout of 259200 seconds. The protocol admin can choose a different period for new vaults; each vault copies it at creation and keeps it. The clock starts when the auction opens.

      No wait while admissions are paused for this vault or the protocol, or once expiry has been reached.

      What cancelling changes

      Collateral stays until LPs withdraw it; nothing is sent back automatically. Deposits reopen if not paused and the deposit rules allow them. The schedule is cleared, and before expiry the owner can open or schedule another auction. An expired vault cannot reopen, but LPs can still withdraw. No premium or fee was collected, so nothing needs a refund.

      Live

      The option has been sold. Collateral is now committed to the buyer's remaining rights, so LPs must wait for settlement to claim the remaining collateral and proceeds.

      What the vault holds

      I · Backing
      Backing and proceeds: remaining backing assets, plus any tokens received from physical exercise.
      II · Premium and fees
      Premium and fee reserves: premium owed to LPs and fees owed to the treasury that have not yet been paid.

      Buyer has paid the premium

      Activation splits the buyer's premium: the platform fee is set aside for the treasury, and the shareholders at activation earn the rest, without waiting to see whether the buyer exercises.

      Claim the premium

      LPs can collect their unpaid premium straight away by calling claimPremium(vaultId). Premium already collected is not paid again.

      1,000 USDC at 200 bps

      The buyer pays 1,000 USDC and the fee is 200 bps. The vault sets aside 20 USDC for the treasury; LPs earn 980 USDC.

      100 bps is 1%.

      Earned payments stay earned

      Exercise, expiry, a pause or an unwind never claws back earned premium or fees. The vault tracks fees, LP premium and buyer obligations separately, even in one token.

      How the fee is calculated

      Feefloor(grossPremium × feeBps / 10,000)In premium tokens, rounded down to a whole raw unit; the remainder stays with LPs.

      minPremium is the gross premium per whole underlying token, before the fee. The total scales it by the notional: 100 USDC per WETH over a 10 WETH option is 1,000 USDC.

      Who sets the rate

      The global rate starts at zero. PLATFORM_FEE_MANAGER_ROLE can set it as high as 10,000 bps for new vaults; the deployment admin holds it initially. Each vault fixes its rate at creation.

      Who receives the fee

      Initially the deployment admin. DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE can change it for future activations without rewriting recorded fees. Anyone calls claimPlatformFee() once, and a vault cannot be its own treasury.

      Transfers carry unpaid premium

      When transfers are enabled, a transfer moves floor(unclaimed × amount / balance) of the sender's premium credit, using the pre-transfer balance. Claimed amounts never move. Burns keep unpaid credit. Zero-amount and self transfers move nothing.

      Terms are out of reach

      The owner's term changes apply only while Open. Once a bid activates, the vault can only move forward to Settled.

      The auction is over

      The bid master's part, selecting a signed bid and submitting it for activation, ended with activation.

      Buyer may now exercise

      The buyer can now use the right it bought. An American option may be exercised before expiry; a European option must wait until expiry.

      Exercise the option

      The buyer or its executor calls exercise(vaultId, amount). The amount is the quantity of underlying, such as WETH, covered by that exercise.

      When physical exercise is allowed

      Americant < expiry + exerciseWindowEuropeanexpiry ≤ t < expiry + exerciseWindow

      The grace window, exerciseWindow, lasts 1 hour by default; each vault copies the setting at creation. With expiry at 12:00 UTC, exercise stops at 13:00 UTC.

      Where assets go

      Assets go to the configured recipient, not necessarily the caller: an executor can submit the exercise while a separate address receives the assets.

      What changes hands

      For example, LPs deposit 10 WETH for a call at 3,000 USDC per WETH. Full exercise: the buyer pays 30,000 USDC and receives the 10 WETH; LPs then claim that USDC, alongside their separately earned premium.

      Payment at the strike

      Callcaller pays ceil(amount × K / underlyingUnit) quote; recipient receives amount underlying.Putcaller delivers amount underlying; recipient receives floor(amount × K / underlyingUnit) quote.

      K is the strike. The caller separately approves and supplies its side.

      Part or all

      allowPartialExercise is fixed at creation: true permits smaller portions; false requires all remaining notional in one call.

      Partial, then full

      After a partial exercise the rest stays Live. Full exercise settles the vault in the exercise transaction.

      If the vault is cash-settled

      Exercise pays the buyer's payoff immediately, using a reported price instead of exchanging tokens at the strike. See Cash: the vault needs a price.

      Or end the option early

      Instead of exercising or waiting for expiration, the buyer and every current shareholder can agree to end the option. See Ending early by agreement.

      Cash: the vault needs a price

      With cash settlement the buyer receives the option's payoff, its intrinsic value, calculated from the strike and a reported market price. The feature is implemented but disabled by default.

      Enable cash settlement

      At launch the protocol allows no new cash-settled positions and has no authorized price publisher. Before enabling, agree on the price methodology, evidence and operator.

      Two separate controls

      Enabling cash admissions allows new Cash or Either vaults and lets cash bids activate. Granting the publisher role lets an address publish settlement prices. Enabling one does not enable the other, and the contract does not check that a publisher is ready.

      When cash is disabled

      New Cash/Either vaults and cash bids, including pending auctions, are blocked. Physical bids may still activate, including in existing Either vaults.

      Existing positions keep their rules

      Existing cash positions retain publication, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwind. Disabling removes none of their payment obligations.

      Enable, in order
      1. Grant SETTLEMENT_PRICE_PUBLISHER_ROLE to a regular wallet, a multisig or a helper contract. The grant enables nothing by itself.
      2. Call setCashSettlementEnabled(true). The set-cash-settlement-enabled command first checks that the nominated publisher holds the role.

      Cash/Either vaults must set a positive maxSettlementPriceAge.

      Publish a price for one vault

      An authorized publisher writes each report into the Hub; the Hub does not fetch the settlement price from a feed or helper. Before expiry a report is an observation; at expiry, one final price.

      Before expiry: an exercise observation

      publishExercisePrice(vaultId, price, observedAt, validUntil)

      American cash exercise only. The price must be positive and validUntil not passed. The age limit, maxSettlementPriceAge, is checked at exercise, not publication. A replacement must be observed strictly later.

      At or after expiry: the final price

      publishExpiry(vaultId, price, validUntil)

      Published at or after the fixed expiry, before validUntil. It is write-once per vault and, once published, can be used indefinitely.

      One report per vault

      Every cash vault needs its own transactions keyed by vaultId, even with matching pairs and expiries. The Hub derives underlying, quote and expiry from the Live position.

      Units and authority

      Raw quote-token units per whole underlying: 2,500 USDC per WETH is 2500000000. The indicative priceFeed can never replace a missing report, and neither can another vault's.

      The price methodology

      The expiry method is a 30-minute average ending at exact expiry, calculated off-chain. Sources, sampling and calculations need approval before enablement. The Hub verifies authority and timing, not market data.

      No corrections

      Final expiry prices cannot be corrected or deleted. Revoking a publisher erases neither its reports nor completed payments. Verify the calculation and vault ID first.

      Exercise for cash

      The buyer or executor calls exercise(vaultId, amount), and intrinsic value is paid immediately. The partial-exercise policy still applies; full exercise settles the vault.

      A zero payout is rejected

      If the calculated payout is zero, the exercise is rejected.

      Paid in the collateral token

      Payment comes from the vault's collateral: a WETH-backed call pays WETH, even though the method is called cash settlement.

      If a report is missing

      Without a valid report the contracts cannot calculate a cash payoff, so the backing stays committed until publication recovers or the parties agree to unwind.

      What is blocked

      Missing, stale or expired observations block early cash exercise. A missing final report blocks exercise at or after expiry, and expiration.

      What still works

      Earned-premium claims. A missing price is never treated as zero, and buyer backing cannot be released to LPs.

      How recovery works

      The admin can rotate publishers and disable new cash admissions while the replacement reconstructs each missing report, using the approved price for the original expiry, not today's price. The buyer and shareholders can also unwind.

      Expiry date arrives

      The exact expiry fixed at creation is reached. European exercise can begin, and cash settlement switches to one final price for this original expiry time.

      Keep exercising

      Both physical styles remain exercisable for an additional period after expiry, the grace window.

      Publish the final price

      For each cash vault, a publisher submits the approved final price for the original expiry. A late publication uses that same calculation with a current deadline.

      From the exact second

      At or after expiry, even a fresh exercise observation is no longer enough to price a cash exercise.

      Grace window closes

      Physical exercise stops at this second. Reaching the deadline does not settle the vault: someone must submit the expiration before shareholders can claim the remaining assets.

      Expire the vault

      Anyone can call expire(vaultId) to close the rights the buyer left unused.

      No payment required

      The command takes no additional fields beyond the vault ID, so the caller supplies no tokens. Being out of the money before the deadline does not permit expiration.

      Physical vaults

      Whent ≥ expiry + exerciseWindowUnused rights lapse. No settlement price is needed.

      Cash vaults

      Needs the vault's final expiry price to price the remaining notional. It reserves any amount owed to the buyer, and can settle the vault even when nothing is owed.

      The vault becomes Settled

      Shareholders can claim the residual assets. Earned premium stays separate.

      No grace window for cash

      Cash expiration is allowed at t ≥ expiry. There is no physical-delivery grace window; remaining buyer value is reserved instead of lapsing.

      Too late to exercise

      Physical exercise must execute strictly before the deadline, so from this second it is refused for both styles.

      Ending early by agreement

      The buyer and every current shareholder can end a Live option by agreement, with a negotiated refund to the buyer that current LPs fund separately. It is an alternative to exercise or expiration.

      Agree and execute an unwind

      Only a Live vault can be unwound. The proposal, consent and funding must all be valid when the agreement executes.

      Four steps
      1. The owner or buyer proposes an unwind (propose-unwind).
      2. The buyer signs, and every current shareholder approves (approve-unwind).
      3. Current LPs separately fund any refund (fund-unwind).
      4. Anyone executes once the agreement is valid (execute-unwind).
      What the proposal records

      How much has been exercised, the total share supply and the refund in raw premium-token units, zero allowed. Also a nonce and a future deadline.

      Consent can change

      A shareholder can withdraw its approval. A nonzero transfer to another holder invalidates both sender and recipient approvals; they approve again at their new balances.

      When to propose again

      Further exercise, an expired proposal or a replaced one needs fresh agreement: propose again while Live. The nonce increments, so old signatures and approvals cannot execute it.

      While it is pending

      Proposing does not pause exercise or expiration. The usual rules apply until execution, and completed exercises are not reversed.

      Funding the refund

      Each current LP approves the vault and funds at least ceil(refund × shares / supply) in the premium token, using its current shares and the proposal's supply. Funding can come before or after voting and can be topped up; one holder's overfunding never covers another's shortfall. Zero refund needs the same consent but no funding.

      Recover before execution

      A funder can take its contribution back, even after withdrawing approval, the deadline or a replacement. Taking it back from the current proposal also withdraws that funder's approval.

      Tied to the original funder

      Recovery stays attached to the funder after share transfers or burns. Already-claimed premium does not excuse a current LP from funding.

      Recover after execution

      Anything above an approved holder's required amount stays recoverable. Rounding up leaves a surplus, shared on recovery: floor(remaining surplus × holder ceiling / remaining total ceilings). The last to recover takes the leftover dust.

      Three equal LPs refunding two raw units each fund one: the buyer receives two, the first two to recover get zero, the last gets one.

      Executing the agreement

      The executing caller supplies no tokens. Execution moves exactly the signed refund from the contribution reserve into a buyer reserve and finalizes the vault. It is allowed at the deadline itself, not after it.

      Three worked scenarios
      • Zero refund. Propose refund: "0", collect every approval and the buyer's signature, execute. No token approval, no funding.
      • 100 USDC at 60/40. Propose refund: "100000000"; the two LPs fund 60 and 40 USDC, then anyone executes.
      • Expired proposal. Nonce 1 cannot execute after its deadline. Proposing again gives nonce 2, and nothing signed or approved for nonce 1 works for it.

      Settled

      The option is over, and no further exercise is possible. Shareholders and the buyer each claim their own share of what remains, in a separate transaction.

      What the vault holds

      I · Backing
      Shareholder pool: remaining collateral and proceeds, minus reserves.
      II · Premium and fees
      Premium and fee reserves: owed, not yet paid.
      III · Buyer payout
      Only when a cash-expiry payoff or unwind refund is owed.

      The option is over

      The option is over, and no further exercise is possible: it reached Settled through full exercise, expiration or an executed unwind agreement.

      Nothing moves by itself

      Reaching Settled does not automatically distribute the remaining assets among shareholders and the buyer.

      Claims require transactions

      Each side claims separately: one transaction claims the remaining pool, another claims any buyer payout, and neither happens on its own.

      A different token mix

      With physical exercise, the buyer exchanges tokens with the vault at the strike, so LPs may receive a different mix of tokens from their deposit.

      Holders call claim(vaultId, shares) to exchange shares for a proportional part of the remaining collateral and exercise proceeds.

      The shares are burned when claimed.

      If a buyer payout reserve exists, from a cash-expiry payoff or an unwind refund, the buyer or its executor can claim it at any time after.

      Claim the payout

      The buyer or its executor calls claimPayout(vaultId) if a payout is waiting in the buyer reserve; the claim sends those assets to the configured recipient.

      A cash-expiry payout

      For a cash-settled vault, the reserve can hold an owed cash-expiry payout, set aside when the vault was expired rather than exercised.

      An unwind refund

      Physical exercise already pays the recipient in the exercise transaction, so it leaves no later buyer payout to claim.

      Expired · cash-secured put

      The deposited USDC stays in the pool.

      Physical settlement · covered call

      The buyer takes the exercised WETH and pays USDC at the strike; the pool keeps that USDC and any unexercised WETH.

      Physical settlement · cash-secured put

      The vault buys the exercised WETH from the buyer at the strike; the pool keeps that WETH and any unspent USDC.

      Full exercise at 3,000

      LPs deposited 10 WETH for a call at 3,000 USDC per WETH. Full exercise: the buyer pays 30,000 USDC and receives the 10 WETH.

      The cash payoff, in the collateral token

      For a cash-settled vault, expiration divides the backing assets between the buyer's reserved payoff and the shareholders' pool; both amounts stay in the collateral token until claimed.

      Call payout, at expiry

      CallN × max(S − K, 0) / S

      N is the remaining notional, K the strike and S the applicable reported price. At or below the strike, the payout is zero and the remaining WETH stays in the pool.

      Put payout, at expiry

      PutN × max(K − S, 0)

      At or above the strike, the payout is zero and the remaining USDC stays in the pool.

      A put at 2,700

      At 2,700 USDC per WETH: 10 × (3,000 − 2,700) = 3,000 USDC for the buyer; 27,000 USDC remains in the pool.

      Historical binding

      Expiry outcomes use the final price for the vault's original expiry, even when the transaction happens later.

      Payouts round down

      Buyer payouts round down on-chain.

      Vault terms

      Before LPs deposit, the owner defines which trades the vault may accept: the tokens, the expiry, the exercise rules and the limits a buyer's bid must meet. Some limits can tighten while the vault is Open; LP protections cannot be relaxed, and immutable fields never change.

      strikeLimit bounds the strike

      strikeLimit sets, per quote token, the strike a bid may offer.

      Rules

      Checked at activation, against the bid's strike.

      Activation check

      Per quote token. Calls: strike ≥ limit, and 0 means no limit. Puts: strike ≤ limit, and the limit must be nonzero.

      Owner changes

      Calls: raise only. Puts: lower only.

      minPremium is the floor on the gross premium

      minPremium is the minimum gross premium the buyer's offer must meet, per accepted quote-token pair.

      Rules

      Checked against the buyer's offered premium at activation.

      Activation check

      The minimum gross premium per whole underlying token, set separately for each accepted quote-token pair. The buyer's offer must meet or exceed it.

      Owner changes

      Raise only.

      minCollateral is the minimum total share supply required before the auction can open.

      Rules

      Checked when the owner or a scheduled caller opens the auction.

      Check

      The minimum total share supply required to open the auction. While Open, those shares correspond to the deposited collateral.

      Owner changes

      Raise only.

      expiry never moves

      Rules

      Checked whenever the auction opens or a bid activates.

      Check

      Exact timestamp fixed at creation; opening and activation must occur before it.

      Immutable.

      allowedExercise sets European, American or Either

      allowedExercise lists which exercise styles a bid may pick.

      Operator runbook

      This runbook covers deploying an immutable Hub suite and running individual vaults by hand. The CLI reads a JSON request, simulates the call, and prints either EIP-712 typed data or the calldata of a transaction. It never signs anything; bids, reports and agreements are signed by wallets or multisigs from the printed typed data.

      Before touching a live chain, run npm ci, npm run compile, npm run typecheck, npm run check:size and npm test -- --no-compile. npm test -- --no-compile test/17-local-rehearsal.test.ts runs the whole sequence below on a throwaway local EVM and never touches your configured RPC or wallets.

      Token roles decide what backs the trade

      A vault deals with three token roles: underlying, quote and premium; collateral is whichever of the first two LPs deposit.

      What each role means

      The underlying is the asset the option is written on; the quote token measures the strike; the premium token is what the buyer pays.

      Collateral is not a fourth role

      It is whichever of the underlying or quote token the LPs actually deposit for that vault.

      Two examples at a 3,000 strike
      • Covered call: 10 WETH deposited; full exercise pays 30,000 USDC in, 10 WETH out.
      • Cash-secured put: 30,000 USDC deposited; full exercise takes 10 WETH in, pays 30,000 USDC out.
      How many pairs

      A call may list several quote/premium pairs before activation; the winning bid picks one. A put accepts exactly one, whose quote token must be the collateral.

      "Cash" means the collateral token

      Cash settlement pays intrinsic value in the collateral token — WETH for this call, USDC for this put — not a stablecoin payout.

      Budgets stay separate

      Even when token addresses overlap, the vault keeps premium, platform fee and buyer-reserve budgets apart from each other and from the residual pool.

      Where settlement proceeds go

      They join the residual pool shareholders claim after reserves; earned premium is claimed separately.

      Requests are JSON with string amounts

      A request file is JSON with the command's fields; every ID and amount is written as a string so it never loses precision.

      Write the request file

      Copy the example files, replace placeholders, and write raw integer amounts as strings; a request normally carries rpc, sender, hub, vaultId and the command's own fields.

      Units

      USDC has six decimals, so 100 USDC is "100000000". Strike and spot are quote units per whole underlying; premium is premium-token units per whole underlying.

      Three commands
      npm run operator -- prepare-vault vault.json
npm run operator -- inspect-bid activation.json
npm run operator -- expire expiration.json

      Simulate first, and never sign automatically

      Without --send, every command only simulates and prints calldata; the CLI reads the chain, sender, destination, arguments and result before anything is sent.

      State can move

      Chain state can shift between your preflight and inclusion; the contracts recheck everything at execution.

      The CLI never signs

      --send submits through eth_sendTransaction using a signer the RPC exposes via eth_accounts. The CLI never loads private keys.

      Hardware wallet or Safe

      Take the printed transaction and submit it through that wallet's own flow.

      Two commands never send

      inspect-bid and the typed-* commands cannot send at all.

      USD figures are your input

      Any USD figure the CLI shows comes from the valuation you supplied; it cannot tell you whether that valuation is current.

      Pick roles and compile the exact build

      Pick a dedicated deployer, a Hub administrator (a multisig in production) and, if needed, an indicative report signer; a physical-only deployment needs neither a settlement publisher nor a methodology.

      A contract signer

      If the report signer is a contract, it must implement ERC-1271.

      Resume with the same build

      Compile the exact build you intend to deploy, and use that same build if you resume an interrupted run.

      Fill deployment.json with RPC, deployer, admin and reportSigner, then generate the deployment plan.

      The plan links their predicted addresses into the Hub bytecode and records every runtime link offset.

      Do not use the deployer address for anything else while the sequence is running.

      Execute, and recover if interrupted

      Fill deployment-run.json with the plan and journal files, then execute; if the run is interrupted, rerun the same command with the same files and build.

      Three journal writes

      Before each submission, again once the hash is known, and again after verified inclusion.

      What resume checks

      Each existing step must match on creation data, sender, nonce, receipt, runtime code and every peer binding, including embedded library links.

      A lost submission hash

      Recovered by scanning chain history from the journal's start block.

      When recovery stops

      Nonce drift, evidence from the wrong chain, or an unknown or replaced pending transaction. Investigate that transaction; never delete the journal or guess a new sequence.

      Plans don't cross builds

      Plans from earlier builds cannot be reused with this manifest-v6, eight-step build. An interrupted deployment must be recovered with its original build and plan.

      What a complete journal guarantees

      The journal reports complete: true only after constructor bindings, library links and Hub administration all verify.

      Starting roles

      The administrator holds DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE and GUARDIAN_ROLE; nobody holds the settlement publisher role, and cashSettlementEnabled() is false.

      Grant before trading

      Grant BID_MASTER_ROLE to the auction operator and MARKET_MAKER_ROLE to each accepted buyer, with grant-role requests naming role and account.

      DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE is special

      The CLI hashes role strings, so manage it through its exact zero bytes32 value using the contract ABI instead.

      No upgrade transactions

      There are no wiring, library-replacement or upgrade transactions after deployment; library calls run against Hub storage under the Hub's own authorization.

      Prepare, fund and activate a vault by hand

      Preparing, funding and activating a vault runs through checking terms, sizing the minimum, depositing, opening the auction and activating a signed bid.

      Check tokens, policies and the exact expiry

      For a physical launch use terms.allowedSettlement: 0 and terms.maxSettlementPriceAge: 0; for Cash or Either, confirm cashSettlementEnabled() first.

      The activation feed is separate

      terms.priceFeed and terms.maxPriceAge configure the optional indicative check and are independent of settlement; any policy may leave the feed unset.

      A removed field

      The old settlementPriceFeed field no longer exists.

      Two fixed choices

      Set terms.allowPartialExercise to an explicit boolean; it is fixed forever. Vaults default to private deposits, so set terms.publicDeposits: true to allow pooling.

      The token list is operator-side

      The request's token list is an operational allowlist for the CLI, not an on-chain whitelist.

      Size the vault and price the strike

      Set a positive minTradeUsdE6 that fits the product, and supply a reviewed collateral USD price and a market quote for every accepted quote token.

      What prepare-vault computes

      Checks the wallet balance against the minimum, sets minCollateral to the owner's prepared amount, and turns outOfTheMoneyBps into an absolute strike, rounded up for calls and down for puts.

      A pooled vault's floor

      minTradeUsdE6 sets the opening floor; the final supply may be larger.

      The CLI does not price anything

      Work out the APR yourself from the agreed premium, the commitment value and the exact duration.

      Create, fund and open the auction

      Run prepare-vault, submit createVault, then have each depositor approve and deposit before the owner opens the auction.

      Read the vault ID

      From the receipt or the Hub getters, alongside the clone address.

      Approve, then deposit

      approve-token for the collateral token and amount, then deposit with the same raw amount. Approvals target the clone, never the Hub.

      Or call the vault directly

      vault.deposit(amount) is also fine and follows the same admission rules.

      Opening the auction

      The owner runs open-auction; if auctionStartsAt is nonzero, anyone may open it once that time passes. Read the auctionId and totalShares after opening — the bid commits to both. Deposits and withdrawals stop; transfers still work if enabled.

      Sign the bid and activate it

      The buyer signs a typed bid, then the bid master submits it for activation.

      typed-bid reads the chain

      The exact expiry, current auctionId, collateral amount and accepted pairHash come from the chain; executor defaults to zero and recipient to the buyer.

      Signed domain

      IvyVaultsHub, version 2, the chainId and the Hub address.

      Approving the premium

      floor(premium × totalNotional / underlyingUnit); for a put, notional is floor(collateral × underlyingUnit / strike).

      What inspect-bid checks

      The real signature, the USD minimum and the collateral valuation, simulating activation as the bid master: role, signature, policies, oracle band and premium funding.

      A separate later obligation

      Funding a later physical exercise is a separate obligation of whoever calls it.

      After activation

      LPs can claim-premium straight away; if transfers are enabled, a recipient of shares also receives the proportional unclaimed premium. File the signed bid and activation receipt with the launch record.

      Cancel and reopen

      A buyer can burn an unused nonce with cancelBid(nonce) through the ABI. A cancelled or reopened auction gets a new auctionId, needing a new typed bid.

      Publish reports, exercise and expire

      Reports set the settlement price; exercise and expiration then use it, along with the physical delivery deadlines.

      Publish an indicative activation report

      Indicative activation reports go through typed-report with kind: "spot", under the feed's own signed domain.

      Signed domain

      IvyPriceFeed, version 1, the chainId and the feed address; the feed's immutable signer signs, and anyone may relay with publish-spot.

      Only spot reports here

      Cash settlement uses the Hub commands below, not this path.

      Publish a cash settlement report

      Cash reports are raw quote-token units per whole underlying: 2,500 USDC per WETH is 2500000000.

      publish-settlement-exercise

      hub, vaultId, settlementMethodology and report: {price, observedAt, validUntil}. A direct Hub call from the publisher, no separate signature; the vault must be Live and cash-settled.

      What is checked

      Timestamps must be positive, not future, and strictly later than the previous observation for that vault.

      publish-settlement-expiry

      hub, vaultId, settlementMethodology and report: {price, validUntil}; publish for a Live cash vault at or after expiry, no later than validUntil.

      Write-once, no correction

      A final price is write-once per vault, with no administrator correction. A late publication submits the same calculation with a current deadline.

      Separate per vault

      Two vaults with the same pair and expiry still need separate transactions and may receive different prices.

      Check before sending

      The preview shows the derived underlying, quote and expiry; check the pair, expiry, units and vault ID before sending.

      Approve a methodology before cash goes live

      The methodology names the operator, sources, sampling, weighting, rounding, missing-data and outage rules, evidence retention and freshness settings.

      The expiry method

      A 30-minute average ending at the vault's exact expiry, calculated off-chain.

      Referenced per request

      Reference the methodology artifact through settlementMethodology in each vault's preparation and publication requests, and keep the evidence per vault.

      Nothing is verified on-chain

      Neither the contracts nor the CLI verify sources or compute the average; synthetic test reports are rehearsal data only.

      Physical needs none of this

      Physical deployments need no methodology.

      Enable cash after a physical launch

      Grant the publisher role with grant-settlement-publisher, then the admin runs set-cash-settlement-enabled with hub, enabled: true and publisher.

      Granting alone enables nothing

      The role grant does not enable cash by itself.

      What the CLI checks

      It checks that the nominated address holds the role and previews the current and requested flag; the transaction itself carries only setCashSettlementEnabled(true).

      The setter is admin-only

      It deliberately ignores role membership; a direct contract call skips the CLI's publisher check entirely.

      Roles never move the flag

      Granting, revoking or renouncing a role never changes the flag.

      Disabling cash

      enabled: false blocks new Cash and Either vaults and cash bid activation, including in auctions already open, with no publisher needed. Physical bids still activate, including in existing Either vaults.

      Existing positions keep their rules

      Publication, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwind continue while the flag is off.

      EOA and optional contract publishers

      The simple path is an EOA: grant it the role, enable cash, and have it submit the two publish commands for each activated cash vault.

      Nothing extra to deploy

      No feed address to configure, no interface to implement and no helper to deploy; each vault has its own stored reports.

      A contract can hold the role too

      It computes the report under its own approved policy and calls the Hub's publication functions; the Hub only checks the caller's address.

      The helper must authenticate callers

      An open forwarding contract with the role would let anyone publish.

      Reference example

      ExampleSettlementPublisher shows owner access control in front of the write-side interface — a demonstration of the trust boundary, not a production data source.

      Publisher rotation and incidents

      DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE on the Hub controls SETTLEMENT_PRICE_PUBLISHER_ROLE, using grant-settlement-publisher and revoke-settlement-publisher.

      No on-chain quorum

      Any holder may publish for any eligible cash vault; holding the role in several accounts creates no quorum. Put a threshold inside a publisher multisig instead.

      Rotating a publisher

      Grant a replacement, revoke the old one, then verify the Hub address and resulting membership.

      Old data survives revocation

      Observations stay usable until their limit passes or a newer one replaces them; final prices survive forever.

      Revocation cannot undo a payment

      It stops future publications only; a missing report keeps the obligation and collateral locked until a valid report or an agreed unwind.

      Check before publishing final

      A wrong exercise observation can be superseded by a later verified one; a wrong final expiry price cannot be corrected by anyone.

      If every admin key is lost

      Publisher governance is lost with it. See the pricing specification for these boundaries in detail.

      Physical exercise deadlines

      For physical options the deadline is expiry + exerciseWindow; American holders can exercise from activation, European holders from expiry.

      Who funds and approves

      The buyer or its current executor submits exercise with an underlying amount; the caller funds and approves the clone — quote tokens for a call, underlying for a put.

      Only the buyer changes them

      set-execution changes the executor or recipient; a zero executor address revokes delegation.

      Cash exercise and expiration rules

      American cash exercise before expiry uses the exercise observation, never the indicative feed; at or after expiry both styles use the finalized report.

      A zero payout reverts

      At or after expiry.

      Who may expire, and when

      Anyone may expire a physical option at or after its deadline and a cash option at or after expiry; reaching a deadline does nothing by itself.

      Cash expire reserves the payout

      expire prices the remaining notional and reserves the buyer's payout for claim-payout.

      Claim and reconcile custody

      After settlement, holders call claim with a share amount, which burns those shares for a proportional slice of the unreserved balances.

      What stays reserved

      Unpaid premium, premium dust, buyer payouts and refunds, and recoverable unwind contributions.

      claim-premium survives burning

      Still works for holders with unpaid credit, even after burning every share.

      Reading custody

      vault.reserved(token), vault.buyerReserved(token) and the premium module's claimable(vaultId, holder); the Hub's pendingPayout tracks only cash settlement in collateral, not unwind refunds.

      If a report is missing

      A cash vault stays Live and locked until a valid report or a unanimous unwind; a stale or missing feed never settles anything to zero.

      Recovery options

      The admin can rotate publishers; nobody can correct a finalized price.

      Pause admissions and unblock stalled auctions

      A guardian pauses new commitments globally or for one vault; a stuck auction can still be cancelled to return it to Open.

      Guardian pauses admissions

      pause with vaultId: "0" pauses global admission, or a specific ID pauses one vault.

      What is blocked

      Creation, deposits, opening and activation, as applicable.

      What continues

      Withdrawals, transfers, exercise, reports, settlement, claims and agreed unwinds; no deadline moves.

      Unblock a stalled auction

      The owner may cancel-auction immediately while paused or after expiry; otherwise it waits out the timeout snapshotted at creation. A bid master may cancel any time.

      What cancelling does

      Returns the vault to Open and clears the schedule; holders can then withdraw.

      Must unpause first

      A paused vault must be unpaused before it admits another deposit or auction; an expired vault cannot reopen.

      What premium and fees do in every emergency

      Premium and fees, once earned, are never clawed back by a cancellation, an unwind, a pause or normal settlement; admission pause is the only emergency control.

      Premium and fees, by event

      What happens to earned premium and fees, and to any refund or continuation, for five events.

      Five events
      EventPremium and fees
      Auction cancelled before activationNothing collected; vault returns to Open.
      Unwind consent revoked, expired or replacedUnchanged; each LP can recover its contribution, and the Live position continues under its normal deadlines.
      Unwind executedRetained; LPs separately fund proportional contributions, and the buyer or executor claims the refund for the configured recipient.
      Admission pausedUnchanged; exercise, expiry, claims and unwind continue.
      Normal exercise or expirationRetained and claimable.
      No automatic refund

      LPs may already have claimed their premium, so an unwind refund is negotiated and funded on its own, never drawn from premium, fees or collateral.

      The only emergency control

      Admission pause is the only emergency control; a full execution freeze would be a separate product decision.

      Not this design

      A full freeze would need its own specification for permissions, affected actions, deadlines, resumption and outstanding payments.

      Run a unanimous unwind step by step

      Only a Live vault can be unwound; the proposal, consent and funding must all be valid when the agreement executes.

      Three worked scenarios

      Zero refund, a funded refund, and an expired or replaced proposal.

      Zero refund

      Propose refund: "0", collect every approval and the buyer's signature, execute. No token approval, no funding.

      100 USDC at 60/40

      Propose refund: "100000000"; with six-decimal USDC, the two LPs fund 60 and 40 USDC via approve-token and fund-unwind, then anyone executes.

      Expired or replaced proposal

      Nonce 1 cannot execute after its deadline. Proposing again gets nonce 2; nothing signed or approved for nonce 1 works for it.

      Propose, approve, fund, execute

      The owner or buyer calls propose-unwind with a future deadline and a refund in raw premium-token units; every current shareholder then approves.

      typed-unwind

      Returns the active agreement: vaultId, nonce, deadline, exercisedNotional, supply and refund, under the EIP-712 domain bound to this chain and the unwind module.

      Required contribution

      ceil(refund × current shares / proposal supply), available as unwind.requiredContribution(vaultId, balance). Funding can happen before or after voting and can be topped up.

      Funding reverts on a share move

      If a token callback moves the funder's shares during funding, even away and straight back, the funding transaction reverts.

      Executing the agreement

      Anyone can submit execute-unwind with vaultId, nonce and signature; the executing caller supplies no tokens. No refund tokens leave the vault at that moment.

      Recover a funded contribution

      Before execution, a funder can call withdraw-unwind-contribution with the original nonce and get the whole deposit back.

      Withdrawing revokes consent

      Withdrawing from the current proposal also revokes that holder's approval.

      Surplus recovery

      Each recovery gets floor(remaining surplus × holder ceiling / remaining total ceilings); the last participant to recover takes the leftover dust.

      Tied to the original funder

      Recovery stays attached through later transfers or burns; already-claimed premium does not excuse a current LP from funding.

      Administer fees and share transfers

      The platform fee is set globally but fixed per vault at creation; enabling transfers lets shares carry unpaid premium credit with them.

      Set the rate and the treasury

      DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE manages transfers and the treasury address; PLATFORM_FEE_MANAGER_ROLE manages the rate, from 0 to 10,000 bps, or 100%.

      Fresh deployment defaults

      Transfers disabled, a zero platform fee, and the admin as treasury.

      Fixed at creation

      Vault creation saves the current global rate in vaultPlatformFeeBps(vaultId); later global changes affect only newly created vaults. Cancelling and reopening an auction does not change the saved rate.

      Activation locks it in

      Uses the saved rate and the current treasury, then records the applied rate, amount and recipient; a later treasury change never redirects an already allocated fee.

      The fee formula

      Feefloor(gross × rateBps / 10,000)At 200 bps, 1,000 USDC gross sends 20 USDC to the treasury and 980 to LPs.

      Claiming the fee

      Anyone can call claimPlatformFee() once, paying the snapshotted recipient; a vault cannot be its own treasury.

      Administer share transfers

      setTransfersEnabled(bool) controls whether shares move at all; a transfer carries a proportional share of the sender's unpaid premium with it.

      What moves

      floor(unclaimed × amount / pre-transfer balance) of the sender's premium credit; a full transfer moves all of it, and claimed amounts never move.

      Burns and batches

      Burns preserve unpaid credit, including partial burns; duplicate IDs in a batch are aggregated before the proportional accounting.

      Zero and self transfers

      Move no entitlement and do not invalidate unwind consent, but still require transfers to be enabled.

      Commands

      set-platform-fee, set-platform-treasury, set-transfers and claim-platform-fee each take the usual hub and sender fields and simulate before building calldata.

      Preview at inspect-bid

      Reports the gross premium, the saved vaultPlatformFeeBps, the platform fee and the net premium at that rate.

      Cash settlement pricing

      Cash-settled vaults pay intrinsic value out of the collateral, so someone has to tell the Hub what the price was. The Hub owns the prices and the publisher authority; an EOA or an optional helper contract publishes, and nothing else is consulted.

      Which accounts publish in production, and how they compute their prices, are deployment decisions and are not encoded anywhere in the contracts.

      A new Hub starts physical-only

      A fresh Hub grants no publisher role and leaves cashSettlementEnabled false; only DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE can later flip it.

      The flag and the role are independent

      setCashSettlementEnabled(bool) is admin-only and emits CashSettlementEnabledUpdated; granting, revoking or renouncing the publisher role never changes the flag.

      While the flag is false

      The Hub rejects Cash/Either creation and cash bid activation, including in auctions already open. Physical bids still activate, including in existing Either vaults.

      Physical-only is locked

      A physical-only vault can never be loosened to cash.

      The flag gates only new positions

      Disabling it never blocks publication, exercise, expiration, claims or agreed unwind for live cash positions.

      Before enabling

      Have an approved methodology and a publisher with the role; the CLI's enable command checks this as a preflight, not something the contract stores.

      Indicative and authoritative prices are kept apart

      An indicative price may constrain the winning strike; it can never authorize a settlement payment.

      Rejected callers

      Unknown, pre-activation, physical and finalized positions are all rejected; prices must be positive.

      Each vault stands alone

      Identical underlying, quote and expiry still need separate transactions; publishing one vault never populates, replaces or blocks another.

      No settlement feed field

      maxSettlementPriceAge must be positive for any vault that permits cash settlement, and is immutable — unlike maxPriceAge, which the owner may only lower.

      Interchangeable publishers

      An EOA with the role submits either publication transaction directly; a helper contract can hold the same role and call the same functions through the minimal write-side interface.

      The Hub never reads the helper

      It never stores the helper as a dependency of any vault; the helper may compute prices under its own approved policy.

      An example ships in the repo

      Access-controlled, with tests for both EOA and contract publication; no helper is deployed by default.

      Observations must strictly increase

      The Hub stores the latest (price, observedAt, validUntil) per vault; publication needs a strictly increasing observedAt for that vault.

      Publication and consumption rules

      Publication requires 0 < observedAt ≤ block.timestamp ≤ validUntil, rejecting expired or non-increasing submissions.

      Equal timestamp, different price

      Still rejected; different vaults may share an observation timestamp.

      Checked at exercise, not publication

      A positive stored price, a non-future observation, block.timestamp − observedAt ≤ maxSettlementPriceAge, and block.timestamp ≤ validUntil; both bounds inclusive.

      No other vault counts

      An observation published after expiry cannot stand in for the final expiry price.

      Affects only later exercises

      A new observation cannot change a payment that already happened; pick validity windows that fit the freshness limit.

      Final prices are write-once, forever

      Final prices are keyed by vault ID, taken from the vault's activated expiry; once stored, every later publication for that vault is rejected.

      Publication window and permanence

      Publication requires block.timestamp ≥ expiry and is accepted through validUntil inclusive.

      No challenge period

      Finality is immediate and write-once; there is no administrative correction, deletion or retroactive change to payouts already made.

      Readable forever

      Final prices stay readable after settlement; the submission deadline does not expire a stored value.

      Late publication is allowed

      For as long as the cash position is Live, without an arbitrary cutoff: reconstruct the approved calculation for the original expiry with a current deadline.

      Never substitute

      Never a current market price, another vault's report, or a different expiry.

      Prices are raw quote-token units

      Prices are integer quote-token units per whole underlying token; for WETH/USDC, 2,500 USDC per WETH is 2500000000.

      What a production methodology needs

      The proposed expiry method is a 30-minute average ending at the exact expiry; a production methodology must also name sources, sampling, weighting, rounding and missing-data handling.

      The contracts attest, not verify

      They attest who published; they do not prove the source was accurate or compute either method.

      Record before enabling

      The approved methodology, the responsible operators, where evidence lives and the freshness configuration.

      Physical needs none of it

      Physical-only deployments need none of this, and passing synthetic tests approves nothing.

      Any role holder can publish for any vault

      The Hub admin controls the flag and the publisher role; any role holder may publish for any eligible cash vault, with no per-vault list and no on-chain quorum.

      Rotation is the whole recovery procedure

      Granting a replacement and revoking the old publisher changes no vault terms and stops future publication transactions, including queued ones landing afterward.

      No outstanding signatures

      Settlement uses transactions rather than signed messages, so there is nothing to invalidate.

      Stored data survives revocation

      Observations stay usable until their limit passes or a newer one replaces them; final prices survive permanently.

      Trust for the life of the position

      Publisher identity is mutable, so depositors trust the Hub administrator and every authorized publisher for as long as the position is open.

      Losing keys

      Losing every admin key ends governance recovery; losing every publisher while the admin still works is fixed by rotation.

      A missing report never settles at zero

      A missing, stale or invalid observation, or a missing final report, makes the relevant action revert; it never settles the vault at zero or releases collateral to LPs.

      Responding to an incident

      If coverage is lost, disable new cash admissions if appropriate and rotate authority; the replacement publishes each missing vault's report.

      A wrong observation is fixable

      Publish a strictly later, verified observation; exercises already completed stay final.

      A wrong final price is not

      It cannot be corrected by anyone; any negotiated remedy happens off-chain and cannot seize existing claimant balances.

      Pause does not block pricing

      Admission pause leaves publication, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwind available and moves no deadline.

      A fresh immutable deployment is required

      The per-vault publication APIs and price storage layout belong to this build, requiring a fresh deployment; there is no in-place upgrade of live positions.

      No publisher, no automatic enablement and no cash methodology.
