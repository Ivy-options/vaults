Ivy Vaults

Deposit assets to back one option, earn its premium, and claim what remains when the option ends.

An Ivy vault is a smart contract for a single option trade. The depositors' final claim depends on the option's terms and whether the buyer exercises. Selling another option requires a new vault.

Section 01

The people around a vault

An option trade has a seller and a buyer. In Ivy, liquidity providers, or LPs, supply the seller's backing assets, and a market maker buys the option. The owner manages the vault, and the bid master selects the market maker's offer. The roles below show who makes each decision and which contract holds the money.

OWNER

Sets the terms

The owner creates the vault and sets its tokens and trading terms. The owner may also be an LP and can fund the vault alone, accept deposits from other LPs, or do both.

LIQUIDITY PROVIDER

Supplies collateral

The LP deposits collateral and receives shares in one vault.

MARKET MAKER

Buys and manages the option

The market maker is the option buyer and manages the position through settlement. It proposes the premium and option terms in a signed bid within the owner's limits. At activation, it pays the premium and holds the right to exercise under the agreed terms.

BID MASTER

Selects & submits

The bid master runs the auction off-chain, selects a buyer's signed bid and submits it to the Hub for activation.

Section 02

Vault lifecycle

A vault moves through four phases. LPs fund it while Open, the bid master selects an offer during Auction, and the buyer can exercise during its agreed window while it is Live. Settled is the final phase, when shareholders claim what remains. The phase details below explain who can act and how each phase ends.

Who can act in each phase

  • OwnerTighten terms.While Open; existing LP protections cannot be relaxed.Manage.Schedule the auction or transfer vault ownership.

PHASE 1
Auction

  • Bid masterSubmit the winning bid through the activation flow.

PHASE 2
Live
  • Market makerexercise(vaultId, amount) inside its window, for part or all of the remaining notional, subject to its fixed partial-exercise policy, exercise style and settlement type.
  • LPsPremium claims are available immediately. Shares can be transferred when enabled, and holders can consent to an early exit by agreement.
Exit expire(vaultId) by anyone at the expiration time; also finalized automatically on full exercise, or through a buyer-signed unanimous unwind.
PHASE 3
Settled
  • Shareholdersclaim(vaultId, shares) burns shares for a pro-rata slice of unreserved collateral and proceeds. Premium and buyer reserves are excluded.
  • Market makerclaimPayout(vaultId) collects a reserved buyer payout for the configured recipient: a separately funded unwind refund, or an owed cash-expiry payout for a cash-settled vault. Physical exercise pays the recipient in the exercise transaction.
Final phase. There is no transition back to Open.
Admission pause. The guardian is authorized to pause admission globally or per vault. Pauses block new creation where applicable, deposits, auction opening and activation. Withdrawals, transfers, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwinds remain available. Deadlines never move.

Cancelling an auction returns the vault to Open and clears any scheduled start time, so the owner must schedule again or open manually. No premium or fee has been collected, so there is nothing to refund. Every other transition is one-way.

Section 03

Premium and fees

The premium is the buyer's payment for the option. When the winning bid activates, the platform takes its fee and whoever holds shares earns the rest, claimable immediately. The example below splits a 1,000 USDC premium between LPs and the treasury at the selected fee rate.

ILLUSTRATIVE ACTIVATION1,000 USDCGross premium
0 bps10,000 bps
LP premium980 USDC
Treasury fee20 USDC

Activation uses the current global fee rate and records the applied rate and treasury for that trade. Later changes affect future activations. Anyone can then call claimPlatformFee() on the vault clone once to pay that recorded treasury.

Calculation
floor(grossPremium × feeBps / 10,000) in premium tokens. Fractional fee units stay in the net LP allocation. minPremium is gross.
Rate authority
Starts at zero. PLATFORM_FEE_MANAGER_ROLE can set up to 10,000 bps. The deployment admin initially holds and administers the role.
Treasury authority
The deployment admin is the initial recipient. DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE can change the recipient for future activations; later changes never rewrite recorded fees.
Earned payments
Premium and fees remain earned through exercise, expiry, pause or unwind. Fees, LP premium and buyer obligations have separate budgets even when token addresses overlap. Unwind refunds are funded separately.

Exercise and expiration

Physical exercise exchanges the option assets at the strike. Expiration closes any unused rights once their exercise window has ended. These are separate transactions with different callers and payment requirements.

EXERCISE

Execute against the vault

Who calls?
The buyer or appointed executor.
Entrypoint
exercise(vaultId, amount)
Who pays?
The caller separately approves and supplies quote tokens for a call, or underlying for a put.
Who receives?
The configured recipient. Assets exchange at the strike.
Partial exercise → rest stays LiveFull exercise → vault is Settled

The immutable partial-exercise policy determines whether partial amounts are allowed or all remaining notional must be exercised.

EXPIRATION

Close unused physical rights

Who calls?
Anyone, at or after the expiration deadline.
Entrypoint
expire(vaultId)
What happens?
Unused rights lapse. The vault becomes Settled and shareholders can claim the residual assets.
Price required?
No settlement price is needed for physical expiration.

Being out of the money before the deadline does not permit expiration.

Reaching the deadline makes expiration possible, but it does not settle the vault automatically. Someone must submit expire(vaultId) before shareholders can claim the remaining assets.

Physical exercise windows

American options may be exercised before expiry; European options must wait until expiry. Both physical styles remain exercisable during the grace window after expiry, then anyone may expire them. exerciseWindow defaults to 1 hour, and t below is the current timestamp.

Section 06

Optional cash settlement

Cash settlement pays the option's intrinsic value, the exercise payoff calculated from the strike and reported market price, instead of exchanging the two assets. Payment comes from the vault's collateral, so a call may pay WETH rather than a stablecoin. This feature is implemented but disabled by default and requires an authorized price publisher.

Two separate admin controls

At launch, cash admissions are disabled and no price publisher is authorized.

Allow new cash positions
Enabling cash admissions allows new Cash/Either vaults and cash activation. Disabling admissions blocks both; existing positions keep their obligations.
Authorize a price publisher
Granting the publisher role lets that address publish settlement prices. Revoking it stops future reports from that address; prices already published remain stored.

Before enabling cash positions, agree on the price methodology, evidence and operator. The publisher can be an EOA, multisig or authenticated helper. The admin grants it SETTLEMENT_PRICE_PUBLISHER_ROLE, then calls setCashSettlementEnabled(true). Cash/Either vaults must set a positive maxSettlementPriceAge.

Publication route
EOAs submit directly; helpers use the same APIs. No settlement feed is required, and the Hub does not read prices back from a helper.
Independence
Granting or revoking a role never toggles the feature. The on-chain flag setter does not check publisher coverage. The operator command preflights a nominated publisher; the admin remains responsible for publication.
When disabled
New Cash/Either vaults and cash bids, including pending auctions, are blocked. Physical bids may still activate, including in existing Either vaults.
Existing obligations
Physical vaults keep their delivery method. Existing cash positions retain publication, exercise, expiration, claims and agreed unwind; disabling removes none of their payment obligations.

A hard boundary at expiry

Cash settlement needs a price for this specific vault. Before expiry, an American option uses a recent exercise observation. At expiry, settlement switches to one final price for the original expiry time, even if the transaction happens much later.

One vault per report
Every cash vault needs independent transactions keyed by vaultId, even with matching pairs and expiries. The Hub derives underlying, selected quote and fixed expiry from the Live position.
Publisher scope
Any role holder may publish for any eligible cash vault. There is no per-vault assignment or built-in quorum.
Units & authority
Quote-token units per whole underlying. The indicative priceFeed only constrains activation. No indicative-price or other-vault fallback can authorize cash payment.
Methodology
Operators calculate the proposed 30-minute average ending at exact expiry off-chain. Production sources, sampling and calculations need approval before enablement. The Hub verifies authority and time bindings, not market-data accuracy.
Irreversible
Final expiry prices cannot be corrected or deleted. Revoking a publisher erases neither its reports nor completed payments. Verify the calculation and vault ID before submission.

Cash exercise, expiration and claims

Exercising sends the buyer's payoff immediately. Expiration instead sets aside the remaining payoff in a buyer reserve for a later claim. Both paths use the reported price, and the distinction determines when tokens leave the vault.

EXERCISE

Vault → recipient

Buyer or executor calls exercise(vaultId, amount). Intrinsic value is paid immediately.

Zero payout → revert

Partial policy applies. Full exercise finalizes the vault.

EXPIRATION

Vault → buyer reserve → recipient

At or after expiry, anyone calls expire(vaultId); buyer or executor later uses claimPayout(vaultId).

Zero payout → may finalize

Remaining notional is finalized. Unreserved assets go to shareholder claims; premium stays separate.

Cash expiration needs an on-chain transaction and that vault's final expiry price. There is no physical-delivery grace window; remaining buyer value is reserved instead of lapsing.

Missing report: the obligation stays locked

The contracts cannot calculate a cash payoff without a valid report. They keep the backing assets committed until publication recovers or the parties agree to unwind.

BLOCKED

Price-dependent settlement

Missing, stale or expired observations block early cash exercise. A missing final report blocks exercise at/after expiry and expiration.

STILL AVAILABLE

Earned-premium claims

A missing price is never treated as zero. Buyer backing cannot be released to LPs.

To recover, the admin can rotate publishers and disable new cash admissions while the replacement reconstructs each missing report. The replacement must use the approved price for the original expiry, not today's price. The buyer and shareholders can also follow the unwind procedure.

Recovery remains available under the cash admission rules and admission pause.

Releases and existing vaults

Ivy deploys new versions as separate contract suites. Existing vaults keep their original code and Hub; they do not move to a new release. The release registry is a directory that recommends a Hub for new vaults. The example below shows why a new recommendation leaves an existing position on Hub A.

New creation
The frontend discovers the recommended release. The user selects a release and sends the creation transaction directly to its Hub.
Existing position
Exercise, expiration and claims use its original chain, Hub and local vault ID, regardless of the current recommendation. Vault IDs may repeat across Hubs.
Registry authority
Its administrator adds releases and changes the recommendation. Registrations cannot be replaced or deleted. The registry has no authority over vault funds.
Recommendation limits
No migration, pause or code change for older positions. Direct access to the old Hub remains. Registration does not prove an audit.
Immutable deployment
Code and bindings are fixed. Defects cannot be patched in place. Original settlement and unwind rules, admission pauses, transfer controls and optional cash-price publication continue to apply.

Section 09

Unwind by agreement

An unwind ends a Live option by agreement between the buyer and every current shareholder. They can negotiate a refund to the buyer, which current LPs fund separately. This is an alternative to exercising or waiting for expiration; the proposal, consent and funding must all be valid when it executes.

  1. The owner or buyer proposes an unwind using a snapshot of the Live position.
  2. The buyer signs, and every current shareholder approves.
  3. Current LPs separately fund any negotiated refund.
  4. Anyone can execute once the agreement is valid, while the vault is Live and no later than the proposal deadline.

In this example, LP A holds 60% of the shares and LP B holds 40%. A 100 USDC refund needs 60 USDC from A and 40 USDC from B, as well as the buyer's signature and both LPs' consent.

Snapshot
Exercised notional, share supply, refund, nonce and a future deadline.
Consent changes
Holders may revoke. A nonzero transfer to another holder invalidates both sender and recipient approvals. They approve again at their new balances.
New agreement
A replacement proposal or further exercise requires fresh agreement. After expiry or replacement, propose again while Live with a new future deadline; the contract assigns the next nonce, and old signatures and approvals cannot execute it.
Funding
Each current LP approves the vault clone and funds a proportional contribution in the premium token, rounded up to a raw unit. Zero refund needs the same consent but no refund funding.
Preserved
Completed exercises remain intact. A proposal suspends neither exercise nor expiration.

Recover refund contributions

Before execution
A funder can recover the deposit, including after revocation, proposal expiry or replacement. Withdrawing also revokes current consent.
After execution
Only excess contributions and allocated ceiling surplus can be recovered. The last surplus participant receives rounding dust; the buyer receives exactly the agreed refund.
Original funder
Recovery stays attached to the funder after share transfers or burns. Already-claimed premium does not excuse a current LP from funding its contribution.
