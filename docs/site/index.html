Single-use · Covered calls & cash-secured puts · Physical or cash

A vault sells exactly one option.

LPs deposit collateral into a fresh vault. The vault freezes, an off-chain auction picks a market maker, and the market maker's signed bid is activated on-chain with the premium pulled into the vault in the same transaction. At expiry the option is exercised or it is not. Share holders then claim a pro-rata slice of whatever the vault holds, and the vault is finished.

01 / Collateral and shares

What goes in, and what you hold.

Covered call

Deposit the underlying (WETH). Strike and premium are quoted in one of the vault's listed quote tokens (USDC, DAI…). Notional equals the collateral: 10 WETH covers 10 calls.

A call is any vault whose collateral == underlying. The kind is derived, never declared.

Cash-secured put

Deposit the quote token (USDC). The underlying is fixed at creation and the vault has exactly one pair. Notional is set at activation: collateral × 10^underlyingDecimals / strike. 30,000 USDC at a 3,000 strike covers 10 puts.

Strike is always quote units per one whole underlying, e.g. 3000e6 USDC per WETH.

Shares. One share is one smallest unit of collateral, minted on the amount the vault actually received (fee-on-transfer safe). Shares are ERC-1155 tokens on IvyShares with token id equal to the vault id, freely transferable in every phase. Whoever holds shares at settlement claims. There is no separate premium claim: premium is part of the pot.

Approvals. Every approval targets the vault address, never the hub. The hub instructs the vault to pull; it never custodies tokens. You can also call deposit(amount) on the vault directly; it notifies the hub.

02 / Lifecycle

Four phases, one direction.

Fig. 01 — Who may act in each phaseCancel returns Auction → Open
PHASE 0 Open
  • Deposit / withdraw freely. Non-owners only if publicDeposits.
  • Owner may only tighten terms, schedule the auction, transfer ownership.
  • openAuction: owner any time; anyone once auctionStartsAt has passed. Needs collateral ≥ minCollateral.
PHASE 1 Auction
  • Funds frozen. Deposits, withdrawals and tightening revert.
  • Bidding happens off-chain. The bid master submits the winner: activate(vaultId, bid, signature).
  • cancelAuction: bid master any time; owner after auctionTimeout (3 days). Clears the schedule.
PHASE 2 Live
  • Premium already sits in the vault.
  • Market maker calls exercise(vaultId, amount) inside its window, as many times as it likes, up to the remaining notional.
  • settle(vaultId): anyone, once the settlement time is reached.
PHASE 3 Settled
  • claim(vaultId, shares) burns shares and pays shares / totalShares of every token in the vault.
  • Cash auto-settlement reserves the market maker's payout; it collects with claimPayout.
  • Terminal. Roll by creating a new vault.

03 / Outcomes

What the pot contains at settlement.

Let D be call collateral, C put collateral, K strike, N = C / K put notional, S spot at settlement, f the exercised fraction. Premium P = premium × notional is pulled at activation and is always in the pot.

OutcomeCovered call (deposit D underlying)Cash-secured put (deposit C quote)
Not exercisedD underlying + PC quote + P
Physical, fraction f(1−f)·D underlying + f·D·K quote + Pf·N underlying + C − f·N·K quote + P
Cash, remaining rD − r·(S−K)/S underlying + P  (payout 0 if S ≤ K)C − r·(K−S) quote + P  (payout 0 if S ≥ K)

Rounding always favours LPs: the market maker pays ceil, receives floor. Remainders stay in the vault and are distributed pro-rata. Cash payouts come out of the collateral, so the market maker delivers nothing at a cash exercise.

04 / Terms

The owner sets the floor. Bids must clear it.

Terms are fixed at creation and can only move in the LP-favourable direction while the vault is Open. A bid that fails any check reverts at activation.

05 / Market makers

Sign the bid once. Approve the vault. Exercise inside the window.

The bid

EIP-712 typed data, domain IvyVaultsHub / 1 / chain id / hub proxy. The signer must hold MARKET_MAKER_ROLE. Nonces are free-form: sign several competing bids, only the winner is consumed, the rest lapse at validUntil or via cancelBid(nonce). The bid master submits; it cannot alter a bid.

Total premium premium × notional / 10^dec is pulled from you at activation, so approve the vault for it beforehand. For physical settlement, also approve what you will pay at exercise.

struct Bid {
  uint256 vaultId;
  address marketMaker;
  address quoteToken;
  uint256 strike;       // quote per 1 underlying
  uint256 premium;      // premiumToken per 1 underlying
  uint8   style;        // 0 European, 1 American
  uint8   settlement;   // 0 Physical, 1 Cash
  uint64  expiry;       // unix seconds
  uint64  validUntil;   // signature deadline
  uint256 nonce;
}

Dead-feed rule. Cash settle reverts with StalePrice while the feed is stale or unavailable. From expiry + settlementGracePeriod (7 days) a stale price is accepted, and a feed that reverts settles with zero payout, so LP funds can never be locked by an oracle.

06 / Developers

Four contracts, three roles.

IvyVaultsHub

UUPS proxy. Factory, every rule, EIP-712 verification, phase machine. All token-moving entry points are nonReentrant.

IvyVault

EIP-1167 clone per vault. Holds tokens. deposit for anyone; pull / push hub-only. No logic.

IvyShares

ERC-1155 + Supply. mint / burn hub-only. Kept outside the hub to stay under the 24,576-byte limit.

IIvyPriceFeed

spot(underlying, quote) → (price, updatedAt), price in quote decimals per one whole underlying. Interface only; the feed is yours to implement.
