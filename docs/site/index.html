    Ivy Vaults

    A vault’s life

    Liquidity providers (LPs) back one option trade. The buyer pays a premium for the right to trade at an agreed price, the strike.

    Call: the right to buy the underlying asset.
    Put: the right to sell it.

    LP premium is available after activation.
    The remaining pool is available only after settlement.

    People and funds

    One vault backs one option trade. A new trade needs a new vault.

    Who makes the trade

    • Owner sets the terms.
    • LPs provide collateral.
    • Buyer pays for the option.
    • Bid master selects and submits the winning offer.

    Follow an instruction

    The Hub checks instructions and coordinates each separate vault.

    Choose a role to see its route. One person can hold more than one role.

    Who calls what

      Where the tokens live

      Each vault holds its own collateral and reserves.

      The shared Hub coordinates the trade without holding the tokens.

      Approve the vault

      Token approvals target the individual vault, never the Hub.

      To deposit, approve the vault for its collateral token, then submit the deposit through the Hub.

      Pause new commitments

      An admission pause is a safety switch controlled by a guardian, an account authorized to pause new business.

      It blocks new vault creation, deposits, auction opening and activation. Existing exercise and claims keep their usual rules.

      Global or one vault

      A guardian can pause or unpause admission across the Hub or for one vault.

      Which routes stay available?

      These routes keep their usual rules:

      • Exercise and expiration.
      • Premium and residual claims.
      • Permitted withdrawals.
      • Valid agreed unwinds.

      Deadlines do not move. A pause alone does not settle a Live vault.

      Open

      The option has not been sold. LPs can still add or withdraw collateral.

      Choose what to sell

      The owner defines the trade before deposits:

      • Tokens and exact expiry.
      • Exercise policy and settlement choices.
      • Limits a buyer’s offer must meet.

      Set the boundaries

      The owner fixes the trade’s boundaries before LPs fund it. While Open, permitted updates can tighten depositor protections.

      Complete parameter reference ↗

      What stays fixed

      These terms stay fixed:

      • Underlying and collateral tokens.
      • Exact option expiry.
      • Partial-exercise policy.
      • Premium-token choices.

      A buyer’s offer must use the same expiry.

      Strike and premium limits
      • Call: may require a minimum strike.
      • Put: requires a nonzero maximum strike.

      Both may require a minimum premium per whole underlying token, paid in the pair’s premium token.

      Which limits may tighten?

      While Open, the owner may:

      • Raise the premium floor.
      • Raise a call’s strike floor.
      • Lower a put’s strike ceiling.
      • Raise minimum collateral.

      Either policies may narrow to one method. Disabled quote pairs cannot be re-enabled.

      Optional activation price check

      If the owner configured a price feed, activation also checks:

      • maxInTheMoneyBps limits the strike’s deviation into the money from spot.
      • maxPriceAge limits the age of that spot price.

      Both limits may tighten while Open.

      Choose the backing

      In a WETH/USDC option:

      • WETH is the underlying asset.
      • USDC is the quote token used to state the strike price.

      LPs back a covered call with WETH or a cash-secured put with USDC.

      Premium is the buyer’s payment for the option. It can use the underlying, quote or a third token.

      Premium is accounted for separately from the backing.

      Fix how the option settles

      The owner chooses a settlement policy:

      • Physical or Cash: fixes the method.
      • Either: the winning bid selects the method.

      Once activated, the buyer cannot switch methods.

      Physical exchange
      • Call: the buyer pays quote tokens and receives underlying.
      • Put: the buyer delivers underlying and receives quote tokens.

      Both use the agreed strike. No settlement price report is needed.

      When cash is available

      Cash-capable creation and activation require the administrator to enable cash. It is disabled by default.

      Cash terms also need a positive observation-age limit.

      LPs fund the vault

      Deposits mint shares in this vault.

      While Open, an LP can burn shares to withdraw collateral. Opening the auction closes that exit.

      Deposit for shares

      The deposit mints shares representing that LP’s stake in this vault.

      For example, contributing 2 WETH of a final 10 WETH pool gives an LP 20% of its shares.

      Those shares determine its portion of the net premium and, later, the remaining pool.

      Approve the vault before depositing →

      Who may deposit?
      • Owner: may deposit.
      • Other LPs: may deposit only if publicDeposits was enabled.

      The vault must be Open and admission unpaused.

      Withdraw before the lock

      While Open, an LP can take some or all of its collateral back.

      After the auction opens, this withdrawal route is unavailable unless the auction is cancelled.

      What withdrawal burns

      Withdrawing while Open burns the corresponding vault shares and returns collateral. After cancellation, LPs use this same withdrawal route; the cancellation itself sends no tokens.

      Lock the funded pool

      Opening the auction commits the funded pool to a search for a buyer.

      Until an opening transaction succeeds, LPs retain their Open-phase withdrawal route.

      Open the auction

      The owner may open the auction directly or set a public start time.

      See the auction →

      How much is enough?

      The pool must be nonzero and meet the owner’s minCollateral threshold. The opening transaction must execute before the fixed option expiry.

      A schedule needs a transaction

      auctionStartsAt is the earliest scheduled opening time.

      • From that time: anyone may submit the opening transaction.
      • Before that time: the owner may open sooner.

      Time passing does not open the auction automatically.

      What opening locks

      Opening stops:

      • Deposits.
      • Open-phase withdrawals.
      • Changes to terms.

      The Hub records the actual opening time and a new auction ID.

      Cancellation returns the vault to Open and clears the schedule.

      Auction

      The collateral is committed, but the option has not yet been sold.

      Buyers offer a premium

      Bidding happens off-chain.

      Each signed offer is tied to this vault, auction and share supply, and must fit the owner’s limits.

      Sign and fund the offer

      The buyer signs an off-chain offer; the bid master submits it.

      Signing alone does not sell the option. Payment readiness is checked when the offer is activated.

      Signed-bid format and validation ↗

      The proposed trade

      The signed offer specifies:

      • Price and payment: quote token, strike and premium.
      • Exercise: American or European style.
      • Settlement: physical or cash.
      • Expiry: the exact option expiry.

      All must fit the owner’s terms.

      The specific auction

      The signature binds:

      • Vault ID
      • Auction ID
      • Collateral share supply
      • Pair-terms hash

      Cancelling and reopening creates a new auction ID. The old bid no longer matches.

      A separate signature deadline

      validUntil is the last time the signed bid can be accepted.

      This deadline is separate from the option expiry and auction cancellation timeout.

      The buyer can cancel its nonce to invalidate the bid. The nonce also prevents reuse.

      Who pays the premium?

      The signed market maker is the option buyer and must have the required role.

      Its wallet must hold the premium and approve the individual vault to collect it.

      An executor or recipient may be named separately.

      Collateral stays committed

      LPs cannot deposit or withdraw during Auction.

      Who enables transfers?

      Shares are transferable only when the protocol administrator enables transfers.

      Before transferring, check the effects on:

      • Unclaimed premium →
      • Unwind approvals →

      Activate the winning offer

      Activation validates the selected offer and collects the premium in one transaction.

      A failed check leaves the vault in Auction.

      Accept the winning bid

      The bid master submits the buyer’s signed offer before expiry.

      Success fixes the option terms and makes the buyer’s exercise right live.

      Follow the live option →

      What must pass?

      Activation checks:

      • Authorization: the buyer’s signature and nonce.
      • Auction: identity and current share supply.
      • Timing: bid deadline and fixed option expiry.
      • Terms: the owner’s allowed limits and choices.

      Admission must be unpaused and the premium transfer must succeed.

      Where the premium goes

      The premium enters the individual vault.

      • Treasury: receives the share determined by the saved platform-fee rate.
      • LPs: are credited with the remainder.

      Backing collateral stays committed for exercise.

      Cancel an unsold auction

      Cancellation returns the vault to Open with its collateral inside and clears the scheduled start.

      LPs can then withdraw, or a new auction can be opened before expiry.

      Return to Open

      The bid master can cancel any unsold auction. The owner can cancel once any one of the three conditions below is met.

      Return to funding →

      After the auction timeout

      The owner can cancel once this deadline is reached:

      auctionOpenedAt
      + auctionTimeout

      The clock starts when the opening transaction succeeds, not when the auction is scheduled.

      Which duration applies?

      auctionTimeout is the administrator’s setting copied into this vault at creation. Later changes to the Hub setting do not change this vault’s duration.

      A concrete clock

      For an illustrative two-hour timeout:

      • At 10:00, the auction opens.
      • At 12:00, the owner can cancel.

      Reopening starts a new clock. The saved duration stays the same.

      At the option’s expiry

      The owner can cancel at or after the fixed option expiry, even if the auction timeout has not elapsed.

      Returning to Open lets LPs withdraw. An expired option cannot be auctioned again.

      During an admission pause

      An admission pause stops new commitments: vault creation, deposits, auction opening and activation. A guardian controls this safety switch.

      If the pause applies to this auction, the owner may cancel immediately. The pause can cover:

      • Globally across the Hub.
      • For this vault alone.

      No auction cancellation timeout wait is required. Cancellation returns the unsold vault to Open so LPs can withdraw.

      How the pause works and which rights remain →

      Live

      The buyer has paid. The backing stays committed until the option settles.

      LPs can claim premium

      The option is now Live. LPs can collect their earned payment while the collateral continues to back the trade.

      Collect the earned payment

      LPs can claim net premium as soon as activation succeeds. They keep it whether the option is exercised, expires or is unwound.

      Gross premium to net premium

      An illustrative 1,000 USDC premium at a 2% platform fee leaves:

      • LPs: 980 USDC.
      • Treasury: 20 USDC.

      Each vault saves its fee rate at creation.

      An LP’s share

      An LP with 20% of the shares receives 20% of the net premium. In the 980 USDC example, that is 196 USDC, separate from its later residual claim.

      After a transfer or burn
      • Transferred shares: unclaimed premium entitlement follows them.
      • Burned shares: unpaid premium credit is preserved.
      • Already claimed: premium is never paid again.

      Buyer uses the option

      The buyer decides whether to use its right to trade. It may act directly or delegate execution.

      The exercise style determines when that right can be used.

      Know the physical deadline

      Physical exercise must execute strictly before expiry + exerciseWindow. The vault’s saved grace period extends both physical styles beyond the option expiry.

      American starts at activation

      The buyer or executor may exercise from activation until just before the physical exercise deadline.

      European starts at expiry

      The buyer or executor must wait until the fixed option expiry. Exercise then remains available until just before the same physical deadline.

      Which exercise window?

      exerciseWindow is the Hub setting copied into the vault at creation. Later administrator changes affect new vaults, not this saved duration.

      Expiry at noon, one-hour window

      With an illustrative one-hour window:

      • 12:00 expiry: physical exercise remains available.
      • 13:00 cutoff: physical exercise stops; anyone may submit expiration.

      No price report is needed.

      Exchange tokens physically

      For 10 WETH at a 3,000 USDC strike, full physical exercise means:

      • Call: the buyer pays 30,000 USDC and receives 10 WETH.
      • Put: the buyer delivers 10 WETH and receives 30,000 USDC.

      If partial exercise is enabled, exercising 2 of the 10 WETH leaves the other 8 Live.

      Exercising all remaining notional settles the vault.

      The premium is a separate payment and does not change with exercise.

      After full exercise →

      When is partial exercise allowed?

      The owner fixes allowPartialExercise at creation.

      • False: exercise must use all remaining notional.
      • True: exercise may use part and leave the rest Live.

      Delegate execution if needed

      The buyer can configure an executor and a recipient separately.

      Permission to submit a transaction and the destination of its assets are two different choices.

      Executor is permission to act

      The buyer or its configured executor may exercise and claim payouts.

      Only the buyer may change the executor or recipient.

      Delegation does not transfer ownership of the option.

      Where the buyer receives assets

      The buyer names a recipient in its signed offer: its own wallet or another address. Only the buyer can change this destination.

      What goes to that address?

      • Physical exercise: underlying tokens for a call; quote tokens for a put.
      • Cash exercise: the payout in the collateral token.
      • After settlement: any reserved cash payout or agreed unwind refund, once the buyer or executor claims it.

      Who supplies the tokens? The buyer or executor submitting a physical exercise must hold the input tokens and approve this vault to spend them.

      Example: an executor pays USDC from its wallet; the physical call sends WETH to the chosen recipient.

      Cash uses a reported price

      Cash settlement pays intrinsic value in the collateral token, using a price reported for this vault.

      Check whether cash is enabled →

      Use the price for this moment

      The required report changes at option expiry: a current observation beforehand, a final expiry price afterward.

      Cash has no physical grace-window cutoff. It can be exercised until settlement.

      Expire instead of exercising →

      Which observation is fresh?

      Before expiry, American cash exercise uses an authorized publisher’s observation for this Live vault. Both checks must pass:

      • Observation age must be within maxSettlementPriceAge.
      • Report deadline validUntil must not have passed.
      One final expiry price

      At or after expiry, the authorized publisher fixes a price for this vault’s original expiry.

      Once finalized, that price cannot be replaced.

      A pre-expiry observation cannot substitute for it.

      See who keeps the collateral

      With no earlier exercise, a cash call backed by 10 WETH, strike 3,000 USDC and final price 3,300 USDC leaves:

      • Buyer reserve: 10 × (3,300 − 3,000) / 3,300 ≈ 0.9091 WETH.
      • LP remainder: about 9.0909 WETH.

      With no earlier exercise, a cash put on 10 WETH at a 3,000 USDC strike, with a final price of 2,700 USDC, leaves:

      • Buyer payoff: 3,000 USDC from its 30,000 USDC backing.
      • LP remainder: 27,000 USDC.

      An out-of-the-money option has zero payoff. Premium stays earned.

      If the cash price is missing

      A missing required price leaves the collateral committed. The recovery route depends on whether the option has reached expiry.

      Missing prices and recovery ↗

      Missing before expiry

      American cash exercise needs a valid current observation. A stale, missing or expired observation blocks that exercise until a valid report is available.

      Missing at or after expiry

      The final expiry price is required for cash exercise and expiration.

      Waiting through a physical grace window does not unlock a cash vault.

      Publication must recover or a valid unwind must execute.

      Close the remaining position

      A Live vault reaches Settled through:

      • Full exercise.
      • An expiration transaction.
      • An executed agreement between the buyer and every current shareholder.

      Submit expiration

      Anyone may submit expiration when the relevant condition holds:

      • Physical option: its physical exercise window has closed.
      • Cash option: expiry has arrived and the final expiry price is available.

      Cash expiration reserves any remaining intrinsic payoff for the buyer’s separate claim. The unreserved remainder opens to LP claims.

      Time passing alone never settles a vault.

      See settlement and claims →

      Agree to end early

      An agreement offers another way to close the Live position. It needs the buyer and every current shareholder.

      Agreement, funding and recovery ↗

      After the agreement executes →

      What the buyer signs

      The agreement binds:

      • Identity: vault and proposal nonce.
      • Position: exercised notional and current share supply.
      • Agreement: refund and execution deadline.

      The owner or buyer submits it. A replacement needs a new buyer signature.

      Consent and funding are separate

      Every current shareholder must both approve and separately fund its required refund contribution in premium tokens.

      For a 100 USDC refund with 60% / 40% ownership:

      • 60% holder: 60 USDC.
      • 40% holder: 40 USDC.

      Earned premium and platform fees are not clawed back.

      What can invalidate agreement?
      • Share transfers invalidate the sender’s and recipient’s approvals.
      • Further exercise changes the signed snapshot.

      Consent and funding must still match at execution.

      Proposing does not pause exercise or expiration.

      Who executes, and by when?

      Anyone may execute while the vault is Live, all requirements hold and the proposal deadline has not passed.

      Execution settles the vault and reserves the buyer’s refund for later claim.

      Recover an unused contribution
      • Before execution: an LP may recover its contribution, including after withdrawing approval, proposal expiry or replacement.
      • After execution: the agreed refund belongs to the buyer reserve. Only excess contributions can be recovered.

      Settled

      The option has ended. Remaining assets stay in the vault until claimed.

      LPs collect the remainder

      Settlement opens the final collateral exit. The assets left for LPs depend on how the option ended.

      Redeem the remaining pool

      Current shareholders burn shares to receive the same proportion of the unreserved assets. Holding 20% of remaining shares means receiving 20% of the remaining pool.

      What a call leaves
      • No exercise: the pool holds underlying.
      • Full physical exercise: the pool holds quote tokens paid by the buyer.
      • Partial physical exercise: the pool can hold both.
      What a put leaves
      • No exercise: the pool holds quote collateral.
      • Physical exercise: underlying comes in from the buyer; quote goes out.
      • Partial exercise: the pool can hold both.
      What LP claims exclude

      These payments stay protected:

      • Unpaid premium.
      • Treasury obligations.
      • Buyer payouts or refunds.

      An LP residual claim cannot spend those reserves.

      Buyer collects reserved assets

      A settled vault may still owe the buyer:

      • Cash expiration: a payoff in collateral tokens.
      • Executed unwind: a refund in premium tokens.

      These reserves are separate from assets already delivered during exercise.

      Claim the reserved payout

      The buyer or its configured executor submits the claim. Reserved assets go to the configured recipient.

      Settlement does not send this payment automatically.

      A zero payoff creates no buyer reserve to claim.
